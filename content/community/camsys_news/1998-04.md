{{begin-newsitem |date=1998-04-28 |title=MAGE Features |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Just to give you a taste of what MAGE has to offer, a list of currently planned features is listed below:

* The ability to design games from three main genres: StarCraft-type games, Diablo-type games, and RPG-type games. This was done because the Reptiles TC, version 2.0 requires features from all three genres.
* The ability to customize pre-provided units, add your own from templates, or custom-design your own.
* A scripting language (tentatively titled S++) will allow the unit programming, GUI programming, and other programming not accessable via the planned Map Editor (to be included in the engine's GUI)
* Custom compression of graphics and strings in new formats that can be read inside of data files, or converted outside.
* Extraction and importion of entries in the data archives will also be supported.
* Forces of nature: Volcanoes erupting, earthquakes, rainstorms, snow, etc are all planned, along with the ability to write spells and/or commands to inflict these natural disasters/occurences.
* Individual units will have the option (via a user-selection box) of either improving through experience or not.
* In fact, I suppose I should now mention that almost every main feature planned will be optional within the engine and the game itself, so that the game becomes more user-customizeable
* Full multiplayer support (Via IPX Networks, modems, direct connections, etc)
* Advanced map properties: Waypoint embedding, 3d terrain, 8 planned levels of terrain, LOS (Line of Sight), True light-sourcing, resource replenishing (i.e. forests regrow when it rains, etc).
* Advanced unit properties: Teleporting, multi-terrain transports, self-invoked morphing
* And of course, what everyone cares about: custom cheat codes... :)

See something missing from our feature list that you'd like us to include? Drop me a line here: [King Arthur](mailto:samuel_l_jackson@hotmail.com)
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |date=1998-04-27 |title=Clarification |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}

After I was informed of a post at [StarCraft Universe](https://web.archive.org/web/20000203102802/http://starcraft.gamestats.com/) which refers to a bit of news taken from this site, I feel I need to clarify several things for him, and for all of our new readers who have undoubtedly followed his link in interest.First of all, Camelot Systems is currently producing a number of utilities centered around the WarCraft genre/universe, including new versions of WarDraft (a WarCraft Archive Editor), WarHack (an Exe Editor), and PudDraft (a map editor), along with developing MAGE (Millenium Advanced Gaming Engine), which is intended to be one of the most comprehensive, advanced, and genre-spanning engines ever divised.MAGE also will be fully customizeable for many different projects, and its first major application (and raison d'etre) will be the 2nd version of the WarCraft II Reptiles Total Conversion, which is geared to closely follow the release of Reptiles 1.0. {{begin-newsitem |date=1998-04-26 |title=String Editors and Compressed Entries |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
I suppose that I should reiterate that neither String Editor/Hacker that we've created so far will read compressed string entries. So if your name entry is compressed, you will have to decompress it to be able to use these advanced utilites to their full potential. To decompress the file, export the entry (#1 in most cases) from Strdat.war (or whatever you've renamed it) using WarDraft. Choose "Decompress before saving," and then save the entry. Import that same entry back into the same slot without compression, and the editor shouldwork without any problems. WCStred (King Arthur's utility) does not raise an error if the entry is compressed in this version. Unfortunately, the editor does not load the entry at all. It is unknown what errors (if any) may occur in String Hacker (Sir Galahad's utility) at this time.
{{end-newsitem}}

{{begin-newsitem |date=1998-04-22 |title=MAGE and future projects |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
We have plans to work on two more games following the release of the MAGE engine. One will involve an upgrade/enhancement to MAGE for a new RTS. The other will involve modular programming that will see sections of a program released to the public as they're each completed. Hopefully this will be a game based on Star Wars, or a Star Wars type environment. Gameplay will consist of:

* The unification of managed-turn-based battles and plan formulation
* Real-time combat modes in space and on the ground in a wide variety of ships, giving you the opportunity to pilot:
   * Tie Fighters (All Types)
   * X-wings, Y-Wings, E-wings, B-Wings, A-wings, and K-Wings
   * Capital ships including: Corellian Corvettes, Nebulon B-Frigates, Mon Calimari Star Cruisers, and Imperial and Super class Star Destroyers
* Controls will be arranged so that you can command units of fighters, capital ships, and fleets, or individual fighters and capital ships themselves
* Land-based planet fighting is also being planned with AT-STs and AT-ATs.
