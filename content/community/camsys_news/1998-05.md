{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-05-22T22:02:00Z |title=WarCraft Adventures: Lord of the Clans Canceled |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Today, [OGR (Online Gaming Review)](https://web.archive.org/web/20000919224230/http://www.ogr.com/) announced that Blizzard plans to cancel work on WarCraft Adventures. If you ask me, I think that's a good move. It would probably never have made as much money as they wanted, and might have even ruined the chances for work on WarCraft III to commence. The full text of the story (and press release from Blizzard) below (source: [OGR's NewsNet](https://web.archive.org/web/20000919224230/http://www.ogr.com/news.html) and [The World of StarCraft](https://web.archive.org/web/20000919224230/http://twos.starleague.net/))

"After much internal discussion, Blizzard Entertainment decided today to stop development of Warcraft Adventures: Lord of the Clans. Following an intensive review of the game, the company determined that given the game's current status and the rapidly changing technology of the industry, it would not be possible to complete development of the game within a reasonable time frame.

While this has been an extremely difficult decision, we firmly believe that we cannot release a title that does not meet the exceptionally high standards that both we and our customers expect from the Blizzard label." [4:20 PM ET]
{{end-newsitem}}

{{begin-newsitem |date=1998-05-05 |title=MAGE suggestions |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
I recieved a few emails today that addressed some issues in [MAGE](https://web.archive.org/web/20000919224230/http://www.warzone.com/Camelot_Systems/MAGE/). Essentially, they were suggestions for the engine itself, and I wanted to let you know what they were:

* Volcanoes (and other natural events) exist on the map, which either randomly spew out ash (covering the map or the fog of war) or destroying certain areas of the map to growth or even the units upon it. In the case of actual land destruction, regrowth will of course take place, and eruptions will either be randomly or (expensive) spell triggered.
* Larger maps than currently possible with existing engines (like WarCraft II and StarCraft). We have the potential to allow maps as large as your imagination, hard drive, and ram will restrict you to.
* Spherical maps are also in the works (i.e. a 3d-sphere where battle takes place... a gradually curved surface like that of the earth, only on a smaller visual scale. :)
{{end-newsitem}}
