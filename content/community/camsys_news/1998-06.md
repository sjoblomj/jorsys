{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-06-08T20:28:00Z |title=SCuM Format RELEASED!!! |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
I decided after a few weeks of mulling over whether to release the SCuM format/specs that I had found, or to keep them hidden until a utility had been created for it, that I will be releasing the specs now, while the "appetite" for them is still out there. Camelot Systems will still be developing its own editor for the SCuMs, but I wanted to get the format out and to the public before we worked on an editor so that you all could know what it is we are capable of.

The specs are by no means complete. I still have work to do on them, and I will be finishing as much of it as I'm able to do.
 
Get the **[SCuM Format/Specs Sheet](SCUMForm.htm)**!!!
{{end-newsitem}}

{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-06-04T20:04:00Z |title=StarCraft Dissection continues... |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Work has begun to decipher the encryption key used in the MPQ Compression of the StarCraft Data files. With any luck, if we can find out what format of compression Blizzard has used to compress the data files, we will be able to write a data file Import/Export utility. To prepare for this utility, we are now researching the format of the many different types of files currently stored in the Data files, like the animation group files, etc. If you have previous hacking experience with MPQ or LZSS-type compression and would like to help, please [let me know](mailto:KingArthur@hotmail.com?Subject=MPQ) ASAP.
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-06-02T03:08:00Z |title=StarCraft SCuM Format further explored. |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
As of this post, I am 100% complete with both the Doodad and Unit lists for StarCraft User maps... What this means is that I have identified the values that correspond to the units and doodads when used in the SCuMs. The format of the SCuM itself is now about 65-75% complete. Once I have finished its dissection to my satisfaction, I will either be posting the format for public viewing, or take that information and write a few utilities based on it. Do *not* write to me asking for this information unless you have a *very* good reasons to. Please be patient with me. :)
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-06-02T03:08:00Z |title=WarCraft and StarCraft Graphics Formats Identical |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
The last few days I have been dissecting the format of the SCuMs, and while I was taking a break, a friend of mine made a comment that got me thinking... what if we wanted to port WarCraft over to StarCraft's engine? I thought it would be a daunting task, seeing as how the graphics for StarCraft look so much more complicated. However, I decided to do some checking into the format before I made a final decision, and to my amazement I discovered that they were indeed identical in format.
{{end-newsitem}}
