{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-09-20T03:52:00Z |title=New Version of SCM protector [link fixed] |author=Moneran |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
SCM PROTECTOR v1.1 has been released. What's new? Much better encryption. [Get it](software/protect.zip), if you don't already have it.

Note: SCM protector features one-way encryption. The advantage of that is that it is more difficult for people to unencrypt the SCM. That also means that map creators should keep two versions of the map: The StarDraft editable version, and the released version. Think of it like source-code and executables.

{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-09-07T04:15:00Z |title=Awesome new Starcraft map |author=Moneran |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}

I just finished making a hot new Starcraft map - Total Mayhem. It's an original map. Different from anything you've seen before. But it's a hell of a lot of fun.

In the map, you pick four squadrons of units (of any race) and battle it out with six other players in a gorey ffa. Every time a squadron dies, you can replace it with a new group of units.

All the units have been tweaked for optimum balance. Every unit is strong at its task, but can be easily countered if you pick the right units.

Thanks to [SCform](https://web.archive.org/web/20000919224208/http://camelot.warzone.com/SCForm/), I was able to hex edit this map for optimum efficiency. It's got dozens of sounds, nine players, and buildings from all three races on one team. But guess what? It's only 74k.

Good luck. I hope you have as much fun as my friends and I have had on this map.

Download it! [Total Mayhem (74k)](mayhem.zip)

(Unzip into your Starcraft\Maps directory. Play on Battle.net with lots of other players on USE MAP SETTINGS only)
{{end-newsitem}}
