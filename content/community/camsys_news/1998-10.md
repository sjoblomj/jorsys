{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-10-04T16:24:00Z |title=Regarding Moneran |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Ok, I've gotten a lot of complaints / questions / comments the last few days about what happened to Moneran. Some people saw a post made by him (and later removed from) on StarCraft.org - saying that he'd joined their staff. That was not quite true. What had actually happened was that when Cleric of [Theory Squad](https://web.archive.org/web/20000919224203/http://www.sclegacy.com/ts/) moved from [SC Legacy](https://web.archive.org/web/20000919224203/http://www.sclegacy.com/) to our affiliates [StarCraft.org](https://web.archive.org/web/20000919224203/http://www.starcraft.org/), Moneran was still a part of Theory Squad, and somehow obtained FTP or CGI access to SC.org. We had fired him about a week before this happened, and I presume that he was seeking employment elsewhere in order to continue to be involved with the StarCraft online universe. That much I don't have a problem with. What does bother me is that so many people have flamed both the message board, my email address(es), and my ICQ UIN aboutmy firing him. We fired him for several very good and justifiable reasons. However, I do not believe that I owe you or anyone else an explanation of why he was fired. Camelot Systems is not an actual company (yet), you do not hold stock in our company, and you are not paying customers... so whatever we do we should be able to do without having to worry about public outcry. Obviously, you are all entitled to your opinions, but be aware that any requests to re-instate Moneran at Camelot Systems,or for an explanation of why he was fired will go un-answered by me or anyone else at Camelot Systems. We simply do not want to discuss the matter, and we do not believe that it needs to become a public controversy / debate. Thank you for your understanding, and that is the last time I will mention his name or anything else regarding him.
{{end-newsitem}}
