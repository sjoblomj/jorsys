{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-11-23T03:12:00Z |title=StatPrinter Released! |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Well, it's been over a week since I emailed Shlonglor my deadline for friday as the last day he could prevent me from releasing StatPrinter to the public... and I didn't get an email yet, so here I go - it's released. And I made a pre-emptive strike at the [QuickStart](qstart.htm) Tutorials page... adding what I included as the Readme for StatPrinter to it in a new section. I will try to do that for all new program releases if possible. So some of you are probably wondering what StatPrinter does, and should I download it?

First of all, it's not an editing program... no this is a different kind of coolness. :) This program generates HTML Tables of statistics for whatever units you want, based on whatever statistics you want, in the colors and format you want. :) Basically you can generate a 280k+ file in around... 2 seconds with all the stats for all the units in StarCraft... compared to the normal oh... 4 hours with anything else? :)

* [StatPrinter v1.0](software/statprint.zip) - (234kb) - Designed by King Arthur using GFrazier (Shlonglor)'s HTML structure on the StarCraft Compendium page.
