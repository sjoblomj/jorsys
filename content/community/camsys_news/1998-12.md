{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-12-29T16:04:00Z |title=StarDraft/Arsenal II Update |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Ok last night I got a considerable amount of work done on Arsenal II, and I'm very pleased with the results. It is now possible for you all to customize Arsenal II to your heart's content. This is especially useful for Total Conversion authors who want to keep track of their own units by their own names, rather than using the default ones that used to come with Arsenal II and SCUnit. The reason for this is because it's a lot easier for both programs to support just the newest version of StarCraft. The newest version of StarCraft changed a lot of the formats for the dat files, as well as changing the dll's somewhat. So I believe we will keep StarDraft SE Beta available for any version before 1.04, and release a new version of StarDraft (and Arsenal II) for 1.04 (and after if necessary).

{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-12-12T02:49:00Z |title=Review: Insurrection |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Warning: This review may or may not contain spoilers, depending on how much you care :)

I went into the theater expecting something quite different from First Contact, the last Star Trek movie featuring the Next Generation cast. Surely the writiers couldn't come up with an enemy worse than the Borg, right? At first glance, they appear to have done just that - by finding aspects of the Federation which seem to be at least as ruthless if not more so than the Borg. Slimier creatures you will not often see...

In any case, the movie began rather strangely... at first I thought that perhaps I had slept through the credits and missed something, because all of a sudden Data comes careening onto the screen running away from some red-suited pursuers. What I can't understand at that time (and still don't) is what exactly was he doing there? I mean what could he have done to aid the Federation's covert operation there that the personnel stationed there couldn't do?

Well in any case if he wasn't there, then there wouldn't have been much of a movie. I was getting worried that the producers and writers would keep straying away from Gene Roddenberry's original vision of the Enterprise and its crew, and their mission: to explore... Picard later remarks on just that subject, that he's not the explorer he once set out to be.

It's a sad moment when this occurs because you almost feel sorry for Patrick Stewart, the actor portraying Picard. Here is a guy who has acted with the Royal Shakespearean group in England, and yet is stuck in this rut of ever more increasing action and ever decreasing plot.

The plot itself seemed to be stretched as much as the face of the main "villain." It seems like they combined several plots from years 1-3 into one big 2 hour "best of" episode. Except it's not even that - it's not the "best of" It's more like stuff I wish they had left alone. Well in any case, to make a long story short if you've seen Symbiosis, Captain's Holiday, Descent, and several other episodes, then you can feel confident that going into the movie, the only suprise you will see will be some woman besides Crusher interested in Picard.

That kind of nags at me, too, cause what's up with the two of them? She's just like his bitch or something and is around whenever he can't get none from the Archeologist of the week (remember Vash?)

I mean what happened to the 7th season romance between the two of them, and the episode where they were joined at the hip, so to speak? Oh well, it's not all bad - there are some scenes that I did enjoy, namely ones not involving Data. Don't get me wrong, i think that Brent Spiner is a great actor, but they're just not giving him anything new to do at all. It's the same old "What's Data's malfunction-of-the-week?" spinoff episode... Data must be using the 24th century equivalent of Windows 98...

All in all it's not the greatest of the movies, but it was admittedly better than all of the "original" series movies. My only wish was that they stop focusing so much on Picard and Data, and instead on the other cast members... I mean hello, there are other people in the cast! The guest stars all have more lines than the other cast members now... It's a shame, really. All they did with Geordi was give him some new eyeballs (sound familiar anyone?). It was a good scene, but I'm depressed that they don't do more with him... especially cause he's the only actor besides Stewart that has done anything outside of Star Trek these last few years.

I'll probably see it again in the theaters before it gets kicked out by other schlock, but if you're not already a fan of the series, don't plan on starting with this movie...the only insurrection I saw was fans storming theaters to get in.
{{end-newsitem}}

{{begin-newsitem |time=1998-12-04T05:23:00Z |title=More HellDraft news... |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Just thought I'd clear up a few things before tomorrow's target release date for the public version of HellDraft:

* HellDraft v1.0 Public will allow users to patch any files except WAVs and SMKs into the game.
* Patching is done using a patch loader which reads from a CWAD Archive that either the player can create or download from somewhere.
* CWADs are created with the CWAD Manager, using the same interface that was created for StarDraft. CWAD Manager will come with the v1.0 Public version of HellDraft.
* CWAD Manager contains no plugins right now *except* an MPQ VIewer which allows you to extract files from the Diablo MPQ Archives
* Battle.net play has not been tested with HellDraft. However, it is assumed that any non-graphical or textual changes will result in a desync/disconnect from the game *if* both players are not using the same patch - basically it should act like StarDraft would.
* No changes made in a patch are permanent. You can have as many different CWAD files in your HellDraft directory as you want, and each can contain its own Total Conversion which won't interfere with any of the others.

I think that sums up most of the common questions so far. If you still need some answers, use the email link above (just click on my name beside the post title).
