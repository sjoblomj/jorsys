{{begin-newsitem |time=1999-03-31T22:54:00Z |title=Upcoming Projects |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Great news is in store for those of you interested in finding out how to program, specifically how to program game editors. Starting around the end of May, I plan to start an in-depth analyzation of how to make a BGE/Arsenal II like program. (or if Diablo II is out, I'll do one for that). But probably it will be BGE. That means I'm going to be tackling the entire problem I faced online, for everyone to see, and with completely open source code. However, this time around it's not going to be just any ordinary open source code. I will be doing a full in-depth analyzation of the problem, its solution, and the creation of the editor. I will be re-writing BGE as I write the webpages so that I can improve my now very silly code. I will start out from scratch and proceed through the entire project (if possible) eventually ending up with a completed program.

Merlin also plans to do the same thing (probably starting in June) by creating a basic CWAD Manager. This will be for the more advanced game-editing people, and will hopefully start a trend of using CWADs when patching other games (because they're much better than Baldur's Gate's Override directory, or the like). His project will also be open source and as completely documented as possible.

If Diablo II does come out towards the end of the summer, then we'll obviously try and gear our projects towards that game to try and solve two big projects at once.

{{begin-newsitem |time=1999-03-06T16:25:00Z |title=Command Editor Canceled |author=King Arthur |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
For those of you who may have missed the news buried in my post last night, we have decided to cancel the command editor, for several reasons. First off, we are very concerned about cheating/hacking as we have I hope made clear before. We would have had to make sure that the Command Editor patches were disabled in Multiplayer somehow so that cheating wouldn't take place. Doing this would be difficult at best, and I'm sure that the more resourceful hackers could find ways around what we had planned.

We were also having a hard time getting work done on it because simply put we don't know enough about the EXE. With WarCraft II, Merlin had a ton of help making WarHack thanks to the efforts of Mordraug. he spent about 14 hours a day for a year just looking at the EXE. Neither Merlin or I have that kind of time that really is required to do justice to an EXE editor.

It also would have been very difficult to use. At best it would have been like programming in C, and since most of our users can't do that, then it would have been useless for a lot of people.

So basically we're sorry to announce that we won't even be doing a Command Editor at all. It's a vast amount of time that we would have to invest to get it working, and it's time that neither of us have. Sorry to disappoint all you TC authors out there who were counting on the Command Editor for your TC's.
{{end-newsitem}}
