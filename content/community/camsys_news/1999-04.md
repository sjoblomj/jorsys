Unfortunately this is Merlin and Dark Knight's project now, and I don't have any more info to share with you on the matter. Merlin's just informed me that he has about 4 weeks worth of heavy workloads for school, so it's doubtful that I'll find out much more from him for a while.

You will be able to change the race of a player without having to worry about StarEdit removing all its units, so you can have players of more than one race without having to use triggers.  Of course, I'll include all the other stuff that StarEdit does, except maybe sound importing.

I think I'll release a Beta once I finish the Statistical Editor, and probably the Trigger Compiler, and some of the map options.
