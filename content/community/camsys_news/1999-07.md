# CamSys news archive - July 1999

{{begin-newsitem |time=1999-07-29T21:20:00Z |title=Stuff |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Well, ICE is going along *very* well so far.  The interpreter is completed to my satisfaction.  It's able to correctly identify and translate all of the opcodes we've identified so far, with one exception =).  I've completed the portion of the program that supports editing of the script headers and all that fun stuff... Now I'm working on editing the actual scripts themselves.  I believe I know of a method that should take most of the hassle out of the project, but I'm not sure how well it will work yet.  I'll be testing it out over the next few days, and I'll tell make sure to let you guys know how things go.  *If* I'm able to get things working, I plan on doing a closed beta test of the program (And, no, don't try to convince me to make it open =).  Then I go into the bug fixing stage of things, another closed beta, and then who knows... you guys might have an iscript editor.  Then again, you might not, at this point it's hard to say =).

And just one other thing you might enjoy.  I just decided to take an [IQTest](https://web.archive.org/web/20000204145125/http://www.iqtest.com/) off of a website that claims that "If the test's directions were accurately followed and the questions were answered as fast as possible, the test you just took usually yields scores that are within 5 points of the professional tests".  Sounds interesting.  Even more interesting: My score =).  I got a frickin 162 (Keep in mind... I'm only 14 years old)!! A 140 is "definetly genious level" according to them, so either I'm *really* smart or whoever created the test isn't =).  I dunno, but somehow that seems a little high to me... No, it seems WAY too high =).  I'll make Merlin take the test sometime and see what he gets.  Anyway, the test is only like 40 questions, and it took me 9 minutes to complete, so if you got some spare time, head on over and check it out =).

**Update**

I think I can say almost certainly now that the IQ test isn't accurate.  I've been getting numerous reports of people scoring in the 160's and 170's and that's WAY above average.  Still, it's kinda fun, and if you have some gullible friends, you may be able to mess with them using it =).  Just don't take it as an accurate method of judging your IQ.
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=1999-07-24T23:25:00Z |title=Updates |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Wow... Been a long time since I posted =).  Don't think that the past week has been unproductive though.  I've gotten quite a bit done.

The interpreter in ICE, our iscript editor, is coming along very nicely.  So far it's been able to identify and translate all of the opcodes that we know so far.  There are still some bugs to be worked out, but those should be fixed within a day or two.  I'll admit, though, that interpreting is the easy part of this.  It will be interesting to see how editing goes.  I'm not going to put any date for release yet, because I know how much I hate deadlines.  No point in setting one on myself =).  But I will say that things are looking good so far with the project.
{{end-newsitem}}

{{begin-newsitem |time=1999-07-10T17:31:00Z |title=Small lie, New programs =) |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
I know, I know.  I said I'd get back to my normal work schedule shortly.  But, really, I can explain! =).  With all the recent breakthoughs in iscript (Image script) information, I've decided to go ahead and begin an editor for it.  Hopefully I will have this ready in time for the next release of StarDraft (which will include ScAIEdit, among other things).  Dubbed ICE (IScript Customizer/Editor... I know... kinda lame =] ), this program will allow you to edit unit speeds, animation data, and some sound data.

I've also begun.. a... well... secret project =).  I'm witholding what it is because I'm not sure if it'll work yet.  But if it does, it'll sure be cool =).  I'm still testing it out, but I'll know whether it's doable or not by tomorrow or Monday.  If things look favorable, I'll tell you what I'm working on.

And lastly, I made a fun purchase yesterday.  I got myself an Imation 4x/4x/20x CD Recorder =D.  This thing rocks.  It actually runs better for reading than my original 24x did =).  So I've been playing around with that a lot lately.  It's cool to be able to play BW and a music CD at the same time =P.

So, yes, SCMDraft is on hold for a little while.  I know a number of you have been patienty waiting for a BETA, and I'm sorry to disappoint you.  Right now, my priority is working on ICE and getting it done in a resonable amount of time =).  So, sorry about the delay on SCMDraft, and expect some new program info shortly!
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=1999-07-05T03:14:00Z |title=Happy 4th =) |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
It's genious really.  I see webmasters all over doing it.  They hide the fact that they have nothing of relevance to post about by writing up some 4th of July thing on their site.  Since all I really can tell you about is why I have nothing to tell you about, I think I'll join in.

This year's 4th weekend was less than exciting for me.  We drove 5 hours up to Minnesota to bike ride.  Seriously, the forest is beautiful up there, but after so many times of seeing the same tree, stuff like "I wonder how well these would burn" or "I bet that tree would make a nice couch" starts to go through your head, and it really ruins the effect.  30 miles of bike riding later ( yes, I actually made it that far... didn't enjoy it much though =[ ) we were back in the car for the 5 hour trip home.  This weekend's fireworks show consisted of a large oak tree with a lot of fireworks behind it that were out of my view, and the occasional "Holy crap that went high!" firework that made its way over the branches to the point where I could see it.  There's another one of those trees that I wish had burnt to the ground.  And if I hear "God Bless the USA", or whatever that song's called, one more time, I'm gonna snap, I swear it! =).

So, there's just an explanation at the lack of updates for a while.  Actually I did get a rather important part of SCMDraft working just a few minutes ago, so the past few days haven't been a complete waste.  I'll be back to my normal schedule shortly =).
