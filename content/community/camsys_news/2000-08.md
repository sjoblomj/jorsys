I thank you all for your everlasting support of Camelot Systems and all that we create, and I hope that you will continue to extend that support and your gratitude towards KramerBoy as he prepares to lead Camelot Systems into new territory.

{{begin-newsitem |time=2000-08-02T21:24:08Z |title=Diablo II |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Many of you have been asking whether or not we are going to be doing customization tools for Diablo II.  Up until now, I have been skeptical about that possibility because of the high probability that the editors would be used for cheating on Open Battle.net.  Still, two days ago I began work on such a program and finished it up today.  The power of the utility was astounding.  Thanks to Blizzard's method of storing the game data, five-hundred lines of code and two days of work was all it took to create an editor that could (theoretically) add units/weapons/objects to the game, (proven) edit most all of the game stats, (theoretically) change the layout of many of the games maps, etc.  I was able to alter the gambling prices, change item drop rates, and add more sockets to items, along with a myriad of other tweaks and hacks.

Unfortunately, it became apparent to me that my initial impression of the game was correct; it is incredibly fragile.  Don't take my word for it though, just look at some of the screen shots below (~400k a piece):

[This took 5 minutes to collect](https://web.archive.org/web/20000818043445/http://www.camsys.org/images/Diablo/Screenshot3.jpg)

[Check the defense are Str requirement](https://web.archive.org/web/20000818043445/http://www.camsys.org/images/Diablo/Screenshot6.jpg)

[Awfully high damage for a Wand](https://web.archive.org/web/20000818043445/http://www.camsys.org/images/Diablo/Screenshot8.jpg)

[**De**flation?](https://web.archive.org/web/20000818043445/http://www.camsys.org/images/Diablo/Screenshot11.jpg)

[Would **you** pay that much?](https://web.archive.org/web/20000818043445/http://www.camsys.org/images/Diablo/Screenshot12.jpg)

[That's a few too many gems...](https://web.archive.org/web/20000818043445/http://www.camsys.org/images/Diablo/Screenshot13.jpg)

Now, many of these don't pose a direct threat so far.  The Defense and the socket increases don't show up on another player's computer, nor does the damage increase.  My most serious concern is the drop rate and gambling alteration I was able to make.  This would pose a serious threat to online trading.  Also, mind you, I've barely scratched the surface with these hacks.  Anyone with any sort of time on their hands could probably do far more damage to the game than I've done in a just a matter of hours.

Keep in mind, I'm not completely ruling out the possibility of our creating customization programs.  We were firm in our belief that we would never create a command editor for StarCraft, yet it eventually became our second most popular piece of software.  But, there's a lot more work we have to do to determine if there's a safe way in which we can edit Diablo and still preserve the game's security.

Any feedback you have on this subject will be appreciated.
