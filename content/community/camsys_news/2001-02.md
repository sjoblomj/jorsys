# CamSys news archive - February 2001

{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-02-08T20:23:35Z |title=Camelot Systems : Jobs |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Camelot Systems is growing!  Job opportunities are opening and we need people to fill them.  From basic news reporting to advanced programming, from creating customs to creating art, we have countless spots that need to be filled.  If you have what it takes, then WE NEED YOU. 

No, sadly, you will not receive monetary compensation for your work, but you will receive a number of other benefits!  Just the sheer popularity is more than enough!  You'll get a direct and popular medium in which to display your work, while improving the internet community as a whole.  Just think of the possibilities! 

More information on specific application procedures is available below, but for the most part, you can expect to have to submit some sample work, as well as a little background info.  Easy enough!


**Reporter**

Camelot Systems is in search of motivated, dedicated people to help keep our news section up to date.

Your main responsibilities would include:

- Regularly updating and maintaining news portions of the site
- Keeping track of community and general news
- Answering general viewer EMail

Requirements for the position are as follows:

- Excellent English and grammatical skills
- Basic knowledge of newswriting

For more information on applying, EMail [KramerBoy](https://web.archive.org/web/20010208202335/mailto:kramerboy@camsys.org?subject=Reporter%20Position)


**Webmaster**

Camelot Systems is in search of a knowledgeable, professional person to look after and maintain our website.  In addition to updating and creating new pages and layouts, occasional news reporting will also be expected.

Your main responsibilities would include:

- Upkeep of all Camelot Systems web files
- Creation and design of new layouts
- Overseeing of news pages

Requirements for this position are as follows: 

- Previous experience managing a website
- Very fluent in HTML and related languages
- Fluent in Javascript (CGI/Java experience a plus)
- Good motivational skills
- Excellent English and grammatical skills
- Basic knowledge of news writing

For more information on applying, EMail [KramerBoy](https://web.archive.org/web/20010208202335/mailto:kramerboy@camsys.org?subject=Webmaster%20Position)


**Programmer**
Camelot Systems is looking for programmers to fill the following positions: 

*Customization Programmer*

It will be your task to create new and maintain existing customization programs that have made Camelot Systems so popular.  You will work to decode data used in popular games and express them in a form easily understandable and editable by the end user.

Requirements for this position are as follows: 

- Experience using popular programming languages (C/C++ preferred)
- Experience decoding file formats
- Ability to work on team projects

*Senior Customization Programmer*

You will be involved in the more complex aspects of game customization utilities, such as creating editing schemes, coordinating programming efforts, and doing some of the more advanced programming and research.  You will also be involved in assisting other programmers in their efforts. 

Requirements for this position are as follows:

- In depth experience and knowledge of popular programming languages (C/C++ preferred).
- Experience using Win32 API debugging functions
- Extensive knowledge of Win32 in general
- Experience working on large projects
- Experience decoding file formats (EXEs are a plus)
- Understanding of 80x86 assembly language is a plus


Although we are currently only seeking customization programmers, we certainly will be expanding in the future, so that's not all you'll be limited to!

For Programming Positions: [KramerBoy](https://web.archive.org/web/20010208202335/mailto:kramerboy@camsys.org?subject=Programmer)
For Senior Programming Positions: [KramerBoy](https://web.archive.org/web/20010208202335/mailto:kramerboy@camsys.org?subject=Senior%20Programmer)


**Customs Designer**
Camelot Systems is looking for talented and creative individuals with an interest in game customization, to participate in the creation of customs including, though not limited to, those using Camelot Systems programs.  You will be responsible for designing and distributing patches in an effort not only to demonstrate our programs, but also to create a unique and fun type of game-play.  You will be required to document your patches to help educate the end users.

Requirements for this position are as follows:

- Experience using Camelot Systems and other game customization tools
- Ability to learn and understand basic customization programs in a short period of time
- Intense interest in game customization
- Excellent English and grammatical skills
- Interest in a wide variety of games
- Programming experience a plus

For more information on applying, EMail [KramerBoy](https://web.archive.org/web/20010208202335/mailto:kramerboy@camsys.org?subject=Custom%20Designer%20Position)


**Artist**
Camelot Systems is in search of artistically proficient individuals to supplement our website and programs with graphical content.  You will also create various graphics to be used in our customs.

Requirements for this position are as follows:

- Extensive experience using Photoshop and other graphical editors
- Experience creating web-based graphics and content
- Experience creating graphics for games is a plus
- Experience creating graphics for 3D games is a big plus

For more information on applying, EMail [KramerBoy](https://web.archive.org/web/20010208202335/mailto:kramerboy@camsys.org?subject=Artist%20Position)
