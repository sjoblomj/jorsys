# CamSys news archive - July 2001

{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-07-25T01:43:00Z |title=Server Move |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
It has come to my attention that our hosts, Recon Networks, will be moving to a new datacenter sometime within the next week or two and as such we may experience some down time.  It should be a smooth transition, but with these kinds of things, you never really know for sure.  We'll keep you posted.
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-07-23T22:33:00Z |title=Virus warning |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Apparently there's a new worm loose on the internet: [ZDNet](https://web.archive.org/web/20021020195547/http://www.zdnet.com/zdnn/stories/news/0,4586,2792260,00.html?chkpt=zdnnp1tp02).

I generally don't report news like this, however today when I opened up my mail box I had three copies of it sitting there, so at least three CamSys fans are already infected.  It seems to me like this whole worm problem could be averted if people could just remember or write down their important email addresses.  Damn technology =\.

**Update:** If you happen to be one of the unfortunate souls bombarding my email with the virus, a fix for it is out: [Symantec](https://web.archive.org/web/20021020195547/http://www.sarc.com/avcenter/venc/data/w32.sircam.worm@mm.removal.tool.html).
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-07-20T09:21:00Z |title=Small Updates |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
I just figured I'd quick let you guys know that I fixed the download link for StarDraft that was causing problems, so if you were unable to download it before, give it a shot now.

Also, anyone who has been experiencing major bugs in StarDraft patching, or in using the plugins, should contact me asap.  I've only gotten a couple of reports of problems so far, so things seem to be going well overall, but if you have been experiencing new problems that didn't exist before the patch, please let me know either by email or the forums (preferably the forums).
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-07-16T06:09:00Z |title=StarDraft 1.08 Released |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Just a heads up to everyone that StarDraft 1.08 is complete.  Along with updated patching support, I also fixed up a very stupid bug that caused TblEdit not to function when used as a CWAD Manager plugin.  Anyway, go ahead and download the new version below:

[StarDraft 1.08 [2.03 MB]](https://web.archive.org/web/20021020195547/http://cgi.camsys.org/download.cgi?id=stardraft) - Updated by KB
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-07-10T22:03:00Z |title=Last but not Least... |author=KramerBoy |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
At long last I get to make my triumphant return to Camelot Systems!! ... Well, not really.  But after a month-long series of delays, GD was finally able to get my accounts activated, and some updates are well overdue.

Most importantly:  StarDraft and StarGraft.  Updates that will promise 1.08 compatibility for both of those programs are currently in the works.  In fact, StarDraft was surprisingly easy to update and is (nearly) complete.  Conversly, StarGraft is proving to be quite the pain in the ass to update and is still rejecting 1.08 patches.  I will continue to work with it, but make no guarantees as to when (or if) I will finish.

That is all, you may continue playing D2 Expansion now.
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-07-08T22:00:00Z |title=A few more changes coming along... |author=Veldhar[GD] |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
As some of you may have noticed, some changes have been made to the Camsys website.

First, the long-awaited Specs Live Wire is now up and running, available at the right menu. It contains the specs of some important StarCraft and WarCraft 2 files for all your editing needs, might you want to start your own editor. These were the latest specs available before the shutdown, but there was more in the archives... that I happen to have ;) I will try finding some time to make the HTML conversion and put them here.

Second, least important but still there, the News Archives section is up. While it only contains the posts of the second Camsys generation, it might be still useful for those of you who have missed important updates or informations.

Among the planned changes, there will be soon a feedback feature implemented in the news post, just like before, so you can give your comments on the news made. On other changes, {{highlight |text=Otak}} is planning to be adding a small new section to the site soon, as well as creating the software pages so you can know the exact use of all the displayed softwares in the Live Wire. Speak of which, more old programs are to be soon uploaded on it.

There is also upcoming projects in progress that are going to be displayed at Camsys, which should keep the activity going, but I will let the authors themselves talk about that when they'll want :)

And, last but not least, the Starcraft {{highlight |text=AI Tournament}} information and signups are going to be available soon! So, a notice for all Starcraft AI scripters, stay tuned and prepare well the script that will be fighting for you in the arena ;)

That is all for now. More updates will be coming!
{{end-newsitem}}
