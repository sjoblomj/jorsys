{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-09-19T08:02:00Z |title=More Info on MPQDraft |author=Quantam |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
A number of people have been asking me about MPQDraft on the CamSys and Campaign Creations forums, as well as on ICQ. So I'll answer all those here.

But first of all, I should say that MPQDraft is DELAYED. Because, simply, I don't have the time. There is one major bug in it that I have just become aware of from two of my beta testers (which does not appear on Windows 2000, so I didn't spot it myself). I don't consider this a problem because I fully expect that I can find and fix it before release. But I have NO free time right now. In fact I won't be doing ANYTHING but school and homework until Friday afternoon (well, I might come online while I eat breakfast and dinner...).

- MPQDraft will not feature an MPQ editor, graphical or otherwise. I left this functionality up to MPQ2K and MoPaQ 2001, a GUI sibling of MPQ2K which is in the works. However, this is fourth on my programming to-do list (including MPQDraft).

- MPQDraft has the name "MPQ" in its name because MPQs will be the medium by which patches will be delivered. I have examined the alternatives, and using MPQs as patches FAR surpasses the capabilities of the alternatives. As for loading them, you can either load the MPQs manually, using MPQDraft (which requires you to manually select plugins), or...

- MPQDraft has the capacity to create SEMPQs. SEMPQs are functionally equivalent to SECWADs, except for all of the advantages MPQDraft offers over StarDraft (this includes the ability to use plugins). But, regardless of the plugins used, it will all be packed into a single EXE file.

- You will not be able to give custom icons to SEMPQs. When I first came up with the idea for MPQDraft (about a week before I started programming it), I intended for you to be able to use a custom icon in SEMPQs. But when I got to writing that particular code, I was shocked to find out that there is no real way to set an icon in an EXE after the EXE has been compiled from the source code (I'm sure {{highlight |text=BahamutZero}} is laughing at me right now, since Macs CAN do this). So, I regret to announce that this feature was dropped.

- MPQDraft plugins are executed at patch run-time, and add patching functionality. That excludes some things like an MPQ editor or ArsenalZero. StarGraft and WarGraft (as somebody proposed) loaders would be excellent examples of MPQDraft plugins (and as mentioned earlier, StarGraft is on my to-do list). However, you would need a separate program for creating patches to be loaded by plugins, which means that the old StarGraft would be used for creating PATs. And, as for {{highlight |text=Fusioned}}'s StarEx, I contacted him like four or five months ago about him making it an MPQDraft plugin, and he liked that idea. However, as with StarGraft, StarEx was broken by 1.08, and won't work until {{highlight |text=Fusioned}} creates all-new data-files for it.
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-09-12T03:41:00Z |title=The Unspeakable Horror of 9-11 |author=Defenderz |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
This day shall forever live in infamy.  Tuesday, September 11, 2001, at 8:45 a.m. EST, one of the World Trade Center buildings was struck by a hijacked plane.  Not soon after, another renegade plane struck the other of the twin World Trade Center buildings.  While withstanding the actual crash, the heat from the fire made the steel supporting one of the buildings reach melting point at around 1,500º F., causing the structure to literally collapse upon itself, killing thousands people and emergency workers.  At 10:28 p.m. EST, the other of the struck buildings collapsed to the ground, again leaving up to possibly 10,000 or more people unaccounted for.  These events were accompanied by two more hijackings.  One of which struck the Pentagon, the center of military intelligence in the United States.  The fourth hijacked plane crashed southeast of Pittsburg.  It is speculated that this plane may have been attempting to reach Camp David.

Due to the aforementioned tragedies, all domestic U.S. flights were grounded, all international flights were routed to Canada, and the Canada and Mexican borders were closed.  Many businesses had to close today, and many schools had to be evacuated.  The New York Stock Exchange had never opened today, due to it’s proximity; however world markets have been seeing falls of 6% or more.

The attacks of today are speculated to be conducted by the international terrorist Osama bin Laden.  The intelligence of the United States is currently trying to find out who is behind this great tragedy, so we may take action against such person and any country harboring such person.  As for as casualties on American soil, the events of today are as immense as any has seen since the Civil War, and this day has been rumored to be the next "Pearl Harbor".

My heart and hope goes out to the people who had relatives working in the World Trade Center at the time of the bombing.  Searches have just commenced recently, which were halted early due to the impending collapse of the World Trade Building 7.  The events of today shall live on forever for us all, as a country, as a world.  I would like us all to pray for the people who have died in the horrific events of today.

Thank you for your time and respect.  God bless us all.
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=2001-09-09T07:23:00Z |title=Fear not; CamSys isn't dead |author=Quantam |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys}}
Far from it, actually. Since everybody is getting edgy, I thought I'd better explain what's going on. There are in fact at least 4 projects being worked on right now. I'll list them in order of their expected releases.

First of all, I am proud to announce that StarDraft will soon be very dead and gone. Why? Because it's successor is here: MPQDraft. MPQDraft, written by myself, works like StarDraft, patching temporarily, in memory. But it is much more than StarDraft ever was. First of all, MPQDraft is not limited to Starcraft; it will work with ANY Blizzard game that uses MPQs. Second, there are NO files that MPQDraft cannot patch (remember how StarDraft would crash on music files?). But the real clincher is that MPQDraft has a flexible, POWERFUL plugin system. Just how powerful is this plugin system? Read on; all of the other projects in development are MPQDraft plugins.

The second project is an MPQDraft plugin. It's called HellGraft, and is being developed by {{highlight |text=Defenderz}}. It allows you create custom spells and attacks in Diablo II, just like StarGraft did with Starcraft.

Next is an MPQDraft plugin that we just started on (myself and {{highlight |text=Dark Brood}} from Starcraft 3D). I can remember a good number of people on the CamSys and Campaign Creations forums wanting the ability to play MIDIs in Starcraft. Well, that's not quite what we're doing. No; this plugin will allow you to play MIDI OR MP3 music files in ANY Blizzard game. Does that sound like music to your ears?

But perhaps the thing which will excite you the most is this last one here. Before {{highlight |text=KramerBoy}} left, myself, {{highlight |text=KramerBoy}}, and {{highlight |text=Ius}} were working on updating StarGraft to 1.08. Well, now that {{highlight |text=KramerBoy}}'s gone, it's just {{highlight |text=Ius}} and me now. But we're still here, more or less. And someday, StarGraft will be coming back. I'm redoing the entire patching kernel, so it should be smaller and faster. But whatever happens, it will work with SC 1.08, AND it will work with Win2K, NT, XP, and all varieties of Windows. And, not surprisingly, it will also be an MPQDraft plugin.
{{end-newsitem}}
