# CamSys news archive - January 2002

{{begin-newsitem |time=2002-01-27T04:31:00Z |title=Technical Support filled, Programmers still missing. |author=Veldhar[GD] |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys |summary=Following to the last week's staff openings announcement, we are pleased to see the high number of applications for Technical Support positions. However, many programmers are still missing to the call.}}

10 days have passed since the announcement, and many applications have been received so far. Thanks to everyone for your interest and support!

We have received far more applications for Technical Support than we had expected, which is a good sign of the continuing activity in the community to help fellow users in the usage of Camsys software and related. Additionally to the chosen staff filling Technical Support positions, honorary titles on the forums might be given to those who have proven to be of a great help to others over time as well! Thanks again for your high interest! The chosen technical support staff will be contacted within the next few days once the review is finalized.

However, on the programming field, things are quite the reverse. Very few applications have been received, and even less are actually 'new' potential staff. They will be reviewed shortly, along with the numerous Technical Support applications. While there should still be enough to maintain a few projects to be announced shortly, we still have much room available for new game related projects.

To this effect, here's a recall and some additions as of what we're looking for as programmers:

## Game Debugger
The x86 ASM language has no secret for you, and staring at whole assembly code pages to decode them is part of your numerous skills. Disassembly or SoftICE experience is a must. Knowledge of C/C++ is a good advantage, but not necessary.

## Game Programmer
You are highly talented in the programming field and have an excellent knowledge of C/C++ or a similar language. Possessing skills in gaming APIs like DirectX, OpenGL or equivalents is a must. You are very motivated in your projects. Knowing how to use the x86 ASM language efficiently is facultative, but a considerable bonus.

## Game Editor Programmer
You are experienced and comfortable using an High Level Language such as C++ Builder, Delphi, and/or Visual C++. You can easily create good user interfaces to allow the edition of existing game data. The possession of file deciphering skills is of a big help. Win32 and VCL usage is majorly preferred over MFC.

***Note to programmers:* Having your own project ongoing is a MAJOR bonus, as our current project pool is limited. Additionally, you must be able to provide some example of your past works as an e-mail attachment, or accessible URL.**

It is also to be noted that all positions require a fairly good amount of free time. After all, you're working here! :) Please also note this must not be seen as an obligation, but as a generic guideline of activity. In other words, it's nothing strict, it is just to make sure you don't actually 'die' (virtually) over time.

The benefits of working at Camsys are:

- Your project is seen by an audience and community of thousands of experienced gamers
- You have unlimited webspace, unlimited bandwidth and full SSS capacities (PHP, ASP, Perl, etc.) for your project's website, as accorded by our host, [ReconGamer](http://www.recongamer.com/). You only need to display an ad banner and the Camsys header on each of your pages, and there's no further limit to your creativity anymore!
- Staff is granted Auto-Op privileges, upon request, at our public chatroom #camsys on [DAL.net](http://www.dal.net/). Additionally, most of our programmers are often gathering there, meaning there's chances you can get direct help from fellow staff programmers on a trouble you're experiencing with your project.
- If anything's missing, you can suggest it directly and we might be able to accomplish your request, depending on its nature. Just try! :)


If one of these positions interest you and you think you can meet the exigences, then we are waiting for *you*! Send an e-mail to [hirings@camsys.org](hirings@camsys.org), and include the following informations:

- Real name, age
- Thorough description of your skills
- Past works example, as e-mail attachment or URL
- Why do you want to get in Camsys?
- What do you intend to be doing?

Due to the potentially high demand and number of applications, it might not be possible to reply to all of them; the selected persons will be contacted back by e-mail with a positive reply. Please accord us 1-2 weeks to review your submission.

For any questions, comments, suggestions, please adress them to {{highlight |text=Veldhar[GD]}} by e-mail at [GoldDragon@camsys.org](GoldDragon@camsys.org). You can also contact him through IRC by visiting #camsys on [DAL.net](http://www.dal.net/), in the evening EST times (GMT-5).

Thanks in advance for your participation and interest!
{{end-newsitem}}

{{begin-newsitem |time=2002-01-02T09:14:00Z |title=Another year, another revolutionary program |author=Quantam |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys |summary=Camelot Systems does it again. Meet MPQDraft, the successor (and hopefully violent murderer) of StarDraft, once again revolutionizing the customization industry.}}

Haaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaappy New Year! Well, it's January 1st, so it must be that time again. That's right, it's time for me to release another program! And do I ever have a good one for you this time. I proudly present to you MPQDraft, the biggest step forward in mod making since MoPaQ 2000.

For those of you who have not heard about MPQDraft while it was in development and later in beta, it is a patch loader similar to StarDraft, even able to create self-executing patches. However, MPQDraft is far more than StarDraft ever was, and will hopefully be the fatal blow to StarDraft once and for all (as a Win2K user I do not like StarDraft). The major advantages of MPQDraft over StarDraft:
- Works on any version of Windows
- Works on any version of Starcraft, as well as any version of Diablo, Diablo 2 Warcraft 2 BNE, and any Blizzard program that uses MPQs (and I'm hoping it will also work on Warcraft 3)
- Has NO slowdown, as StarDraft used to
- Has NO files which it cannot patch (like StarDraft couldn't patch music files)
- Has no known bugs at this time (after being beta-tested for a while)
- But perhaps the biggest advantage is that MPQDraft supports patching plugins (i.e. StarGraft will be a plugin soon)

However, unlike StarDraft, MPQDraft does not include a patch creator. MPQDraft runs MPQ patches created by [MPQ2K](http://www.campaigncreations.com/starcraft/mpq2k/). In addition, MPQDraft will also run Diablo 2 Mod Manager mods (D2M files) just as well. But, unlike a normal mod created with MPQ2K, you do not need to replace patch_rt.mpq or ANY existing MPQ. You can even run multiple patches at once.

Anyways, enough babbling. Click here to download [MPQDraft](http://www.camsys.org/download.php?id=12) (Yes I know it doesn't have a manual yet. I'll get to it)
{{end-newsitem}}


{{begin-newsitem |time=2002-01-01T05:30:00Z |title=Welcome to the new Castle of Camelot! |author=Veldhar[GD] |site=Camsys |categories=Camsys |summary=After long months of reconstruction, managing, planning and development, Camelot Systems is now ready to unveil its new website and philosophy!}}

It took much time and efforts to bring Camelot Systems back online, but they have not been in vain. Welcome to the new Castle of Camelot!

Many changes have been done, and little is the same as before, except our name and devotion which are all the same: a complete dedication to the gaming world.

For many of you who have been visiting Camelot Systems during the past months, or even years, it has been known as being the reference for StarCraft customization software, and somewhat less for Descent 3, and a few other games. However, the new Camelot Systems is taking a new turn, as StarCraft has been declining for a long time due to its age, and the near arrival of WarCraft 3. This reason has led to the complete reconstruction of Camelot Systems, which is now restarting from scratch: website, and staff.

Camelot Systems will now be openly supporting most kinds of game-related projects: while keeping an important place to game customization software as we did for the past years, another important one will be made for serious game projects development done by the whole gaming community.

We will be running a massive hiring period during the following weeks, intended for all developers and/or leaders having *serious* customization software or game projects. It is to be noted that these projects should not be commercial intended, and should **not** be taken too lightly either; we do not want one-week-until-failure projects. While this will not be an obligation to finish your project, it is done to ensure we are only maintaining and promoting real, promising projects. More information on hirings will be given in the next few days. **Please do not start sending your applications now, or they will be ignored!**

As for our website, many features are working, and others are left for future soon development. Available are our software archives, survey(please vote!), account registration, news comments, and last but not least, our revamped forums, which are now directly part of the site's codebase. Feel free to take a look around and participate in the new forums. Shall you find bugs, please [e-mail me](mailto:GoldDragon@camsys.org?subject=Bug Report) with a thorough description of what you did and what occured, so I may fix them as soon as possible.

Happy new year to all our visitors, and may the new dynasty of Camelot start on this first day of 2002!
{{end-newsitem}}
