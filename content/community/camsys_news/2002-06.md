It appears that our hosts, ReconGamer, have proceeded to a possible ISP change, which has also brought a complete change of IPs. As our DNS is still pointing to the old address, our website is currently unavailable through the normal http://www.camsys.org/ address.

Mutant of the ORG network is maintaining the DNS himself. As soon as we can contact him, everything should be back to normal. As Mutant is currently unavailable, if you know or have any contact to him, please ask him to check his e-mail at warcraft.org for further information.

During the meantime, please pass the word to those you know in the Camsys community.

Sorry for the inconvenience, and thanks for your cooperation.
