StatPrinter Released! - - 22:12 EST
  • Well, it's been over a week since I emailed Shlonglor my deadline for friday as the last day he could prevent me from releasing StatPrinter to the public... and I didn't get an email yet, so here I go - it's released. And I made a pre-emptive strike at the QuickStart Tutorials page... adding what I included as the Readme for StatPrinter to it in a new section. I will try to do that for all new program releases if possible. So some of you are probably wondering what StatPrinter does, and should I download it?
