Camelot Systems gets its own domain! - - 16:57 EST
  • Well I figured that after a very successful second year for Camelot Systems, it was about time we had our own domain. So thanks to the generosity of BetaMax and the rest of the Warzone staff, we now have one! www.camsys.org will be our new address effective within the next day or two. Once the domain goes active I will place a redirection message here (at www.camsys.org). I will have Warzone keep the old address active for about 2 weeks, after which time we will be permanently situated at www.camsys.org. Why ".org" you ask? Well to be honest it sounds less intimidating than ".com" and describes us better in any case - we're definately a non-profit organization, not a real company, so the org seemed most appropriate.

    In addition, now that we're going to have our own domain and be covering many additional games, I thought it was about time that we break up the site a bit. The main page will still be news about all of the games we customize, etc, but I will be splitting up the site based on the games we cover. In other words, StarDraft and the other StarCraft tools will be moved to something like starcraft.camsys.org and that particular webpage will cover only starcraft related stuff. That way it's easier for everyone to find news and files for the games they're interested in rather than having to wade through news and files for games you don't like.

    So since we will be having a great many changes take place, please be patient with us and if portions of the site start malfunctioning, my apologies in advance. I will also be looking for assistant webmasters, graphics artists, etc to help with the page expansion. I'd like to give each special game page its own unique feel, etc.
The Return (sort of) - - 10:40 EST
  • I'm very pleased to be able to announce my return to working at Camelot Systems. The summer I spent working for Blizzard was the best time of my life, and I eagerly await next summer now that I've been invited back :) I do have plans to work outside of CamSys during my upcoming Sophomore year at college. With any luck I'll be able to do that and keep programming custom utilities for some of the best games out there.

    Unfortunately a bit of bad news first: effective immediately I'm abandoning the Lead Webmaster position here in favor of programming productivity. As such, KramerBoy has been promoted to the Lead Webmaster position until such time as I decide I miss the job (not likely) or he decides he's had enough. I'll still be making posts now and again, but the bulk of the news you see should be from KramerBoy.

    Also, as much as it might pain some of us to admit it, StarCraft is dying if not dead already. At least in the US. Korea it appears will keep it alive for a while longer, but for now all of us at CamSys realize that we have to move on to new games and new custom utilities. Therefore I've recently purchased copies of Braveheart, DarkStone and Tiberian Sun in hopes that I can create customization utilities for some if not all of these games. The reason for the selection of these games is fairly straightforeward:
    • Braveheart - Loved that movie, have to try and customize it :)
    • DarkStone - Took a look at this one during the summer. I already have an archive editor that I'll be releasing as soon as I put it through a few more tests and add a couple of features. No real personal interest in this game, just I saw how easy the archives would be to edit and couldn't resist :P
    • Tiberian Sun - By popular demand I've purchased this game in hopes that I can customize it for the many people who asked me to try. Haven't ever played a C&C; game before so I'm not quite sure how it will compare to other C&C;'s and to SC.

    Why the sudden change to non-Blizzard games? Basically because I've worked there and am not allowed to. Although Diablo II will be out soon, I don't anticipate a large demand for customization of that game, so I don't think we'd even bother anyways.

    Anyways that's my agenda for now. I can't guarantee that if you request other games to customize I'll be able to get copies of them. I'll probably stick with these three for a while anywho. In any case, good to be back, and I'll keep you all up to date on my progress.
    One final quick note: we may have a new layout up in a few days. KB and I will be trying to impliment it this week and see how it looks.
