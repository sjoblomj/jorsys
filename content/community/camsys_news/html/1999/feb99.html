Command Editor: A Request - - 17:29 EST
  • I'm only going to say this once: please stop emailing me about the Command Editor.
    1. Merlin hasn't even finished it yet, so answering any questions about its availability is pointless.
    2. Don't ask me to put you on a list to get it emailed to you. For most of you asking this, I realize that you probably have a legitimate interest in the Command Editor, but to be honest there's no way for me to tell the difference between you and Alyssa P. Hacker.
    3. Please don't ask me how cheating would be possible. If we do finish the Command Editor, the answers to that question will only make things worse. Also, it's nearly impossible to predict all the methods of cheating that are available through a utility that isn't even completed yet.
    4. If you have questions/concerns/comments, please use the forum. My email address is not a discussion forum, so don't use it as one. We have 3 free forums for you to post any comments you want on there with no limits except profanity, so please, share your comments with the forums. That way hopefully the massive number of redundant emails I've been getting that all ask the same question can be answered in one (or three in this case) location.
    5. The reason that we're concerned about cheating is that we don't want StarCraft to be destroyed like Diablo's multiplayer aspect was when people cheated there.
    6. Don't bribe me. First off, even if we did want the money, we couldn't sell our programs cause then Blizzard would sue our asses into the ground. And I'm sure that most of you don't want that to happen.

    Any more emails that I get about the Command Editor will probably be ignored/deleted, so if you don't get a response to your message, don't be too suprised.

About the Command Editor - - 21:05 EST
  • I got a lot of email today about the Command Editor. A lot of people have problems seeing how the Command Editor could be used for cheating, or would rather have it released even if it does allow cheating just to make sure that Total Conversions have access to using it.
    I think I owe you an explanation. The Command Editor is capable of a lot more than its name suggests (which isn't going to be its final name anyways). While creating cheats is of course not its main goal, it could probably be used to do that by people if they wanted to. One solution is of course to limit the release of the Command Editor to only people who email me for it, or a selected group of insiders, etc. That's obviously not going to be a very popular move on our part, and it probably won't be very effective either, cause there's no way of us making sure that it doesn't leak out to the public. I'm sure this is the kind of thing that sites like (*name removed to protect the guilty*) would just love to get their hands on and then put it up on their site where we can do nothing to take it down.
    It's quite possible that the Command Editor will never even get finished. Regardless of what happens, please keep one thing in mind: there are reasons for pretty much everything we do, including trying to prevent cheating with the Command Editor. Most of them have to do with preserving the integrity of a game like StarCraft for as long as possible. We certainly don't want to be responsible for the total collapse of Battle.net due to large amounts of cheating. So please bear with us and at least respect our decisions, even if you don't like them. After all, I'll say it once again just for those of you who might have eye problems, our software is free. We'll do what we want with it and there's not a whole heck of a lot you can do to change our minds. Not until you're paying our bills at least.
