Command Editor Canceled - - 11:25 EST
  • For those of you who may have missed the news buried in my post last night, we have decided to cancel the command editor, for several reasons. First off, we are very concerned about cheating/hacking as we have I hope made clear before. We would have had to make sure that the Command Editor patches were disabled in Multiplayer somehow so that cheating wouldn't take place. Doing this would be difficult at best, and I'm sure that the more resourceful hackers could find ways around what we had planned.
    We were also having a hard time getting work done on it because simply put we don't know enough about the EXE. With WarCraft II, Merlin had a ton of help making WarHack thanks to the efforts of Mordraug. he spent about 14 hours a day for a year just looking at the EXE. Neither Merlin or I have that kind of time that really is required to do justice to an EXE editor.
    It also would have been very difficult to use. At best it would have been like programming in C, and since most of our users can't do that, then it would have been useless for a lot of people.
    So basically we're sorry to announce that we won't even be doing a Command Editor at all. It's a vast amount of time that we would have to invest to get it working, and it's time that neither of us have. Sorry to disappoint all you TC authors out there who were counting on the Command Editor for your TC's.
