Friday August 25, 2000
A 'new' direction is taken(at least for me)Gold Dragon @ 12:58 EST

A completely off-topic introduction, my human form is currently in college, at a computer lab. Mostly killing time, as it lasts 3 hours(up to 3PM), and we only had to type an easy document to "get used to Word". Obviously I finished it in 10 minutes(there was a good amount of text to type). Then I thought, why not spend some time at Camsys? Which is what I'm doing basically ;)

From the amount of responses I got from my last post, I've got a fairly good interest at the programming subject, which was equaled by Diablo 2, but because of some reasons I mentionned in the feedbacks, this is unfortunately not possible for now. However, I'm more than interested in developing the programming subject, mostly taking care of the programming section & board we'll have(the board is already up, check it out!). I'll also write some tutorials on this subject, although I don't know whether I should concentrate writing basic or advanced stuff... That's why I'll once again ask for what kind of programming topic you'd like to see ;) This way I might be able to start writing some documents, and everyone else wanting to participate is welcome too. Speak of suggestions, forget ultra-advanced stuff like "Making Quake IV from scratch", I won't accept such things, for an obvious reason. This is way too easy, ask something harder ;) j/k

So that's basically the goal I'll aim for a while. Some may wonder what's the state of my released projects at Camsys... I consider them finished. Not in the sense 'perfect', but in the sense 'I think they're doing the job, and are stable enough to be used without problems'. In other words, I won't go through new features and such for them. At least for now. My only 'public' priorities right now are taking care of Camsys, working on my personal/secret project and now, the programming section. This last one will be up soon, when I'll have at least a document ready I guess ;) Until then, don't hesitate to suggest topics you'd like to see treated, that will be of a great help. Don't forget to use the new board either :)

That's enough for now, I'll kill some other time in the boards :) Until next update!

Thursday August 17, 2000
*opens wings and gives a sign of life*Gold Dragon @ 23:22 EST

It's been quite a while I haven't given a single bit of news... It makes 18 days tonight. I thought perhaps it would be good to get rid of that "inactivity", to let you know I'm still alive :)

I'll start immediately with the sad news for Camelot Systems, about KA's departure as you probably read below. This is indeed a big loss for Camelot Systems, as he did a wonderful job with customization softwares, and many projects wouldn't have been possible without his work. Thanks King Arthur for your great help to the SC community, you'll be missed! Although it's not THAT sad, since he seems to have fun(and probably lots of work) with his Blizzard job :) Good luck at Blizzard!

Now you might wonder what I've did during these 18 days? Actually, I've done a lot, but nothing related to Camsys. In fact, I've been taking care of my last days of vacation, so I took some "vacations" from Camsys. This means, I haven't replied any e-mails during that time(I sincerely apologize) and this is the same for the forums and other Camsys-related things. But all in all, since ScAIEdit 3.1 seems to be stable overall, I don't think you have suffered much on it ;)

This said, it brings an interesting question. Now that my biggest project has been achieved(I'll probably do a little maintenance update, but there won't be new things added, as far as I consider), I'm now with no projects. Or is it? Yes, I'm currently developing a personal project which will remain secret for a bit of time until it's advanced enough to risk unveiling it(although many already know, but it's not that important :). But concretely, it's still not a Camsys project. I've been thinking a bit of it, and we can no longer hide the fact SC customization software is reaching an end, except for Mutagen being developed by KramerBoy, which is the new leader(congrats!). Just before you say SC customization isn't dying, please notice I was talking about softwares :)

This brings a really interesting question : What new goal should Camsys head to? I'm not asking you to answer(although you're welcome to suggest if you want). Should we head to customize another popular game(as we already tried in the past), or start writing programming tutorials as KA did some time ago? This is an example of the decisions hard to take for now. It's most likely we'll have to choose a path, but there is no urge to decide now. The "normal" path would have been to design Diablo 2 customization tools, but the cheating issue is so big we can hardly do that without helping cheaters. So...

I guess this completes what I wanted to say on that subject. So what am I going to do? Probably work on my personal project and devote more time to college(which is starting Monday). :) Many have been asking of the AI Tournament I planned to organize, and this is a good question. Since time will surely start to be even more precious next week, it's probable I'll leak time to organize it. Knowing this, I might end up proposing someone I know to take care of it, as I think it's an idea worth to be done :) I still have to think of it, so please don't ask for tournament update until I take a clear decision :)

I'll start getting more active by replying my e-mails and taking care of the things I'm used to in the next days :) With no Camsys project from me on the works for now, this should actually free up some time ;) So, until next update...
