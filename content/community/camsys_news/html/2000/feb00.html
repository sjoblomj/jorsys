Tuesday, February 29, 2000
Change of plans...King Arthur @ 17:57 EST

I'm taking a physics course this semester on Relativity & Cosmology. Fortunately this course has a final project rather than a final :) In any case for this final project I've decided to do a "true-physics" first person edition of the classic game Asteroids. This will be my first real game, and I want to spend a lot of time on it because I want it to be as good as possible. As a result, you probably won't see much in the way of custom tools from me except maybe over my Spring break (the 11th - 19th of March). This is a big change from my normal day to day activities. I think that game programming appeals to a lot of you, so I will try to document my progress and post updates/screenshots/demos as things develop. I'll probably end up making the entire thing Open-Source because of this, which is fine with me because I have to turn in the source code when the project is done anyways... So I thought I should let you know what I'm up to, and to hopefully keep you interested in checking up on my progress. The code-name for the project right now is Nova... which may change after a while if I come up with something better. The advantage of that name is that it lends itself very well to a sequel... a la SuperNova :P doh I'd better stop before I get flamed. In any case, the webpage for the project will be located here, and I will post updates there as needed.

Friday, February 25, 2000
ScAIEdit updateDark Knight @ 22:41 EST

Looks like I'm still not used to post regularly on the projects I'm currently working on :) I just hate to appear inactive when in fact I'm far, really far from that ;) So here we go...

These last times, lots of things happened, in Real Life(tm), in my projects, and some others... Speaking of projects, as I write this, I'm currently working on three(!) projects, that you maybe don't know. In addition to work on ScAIEdit III, I also work on Frag Tag, as well as EarthBound, both of them being Descent 3 MODs. So as you can see, I've got a pretty good reason to say I've got little time. I shouldn't have taken so much? Maybe... But since Descent 3 MODding is on pretty early stages, if I can help it to get started, I don't see why I wouldn't ;) Fortunately enough, Frag Tag is almost finished(99%), and the other one shouldn't take too long, hopefully.

I haven't neglected ScAIEdit III however. The only reason why it appears to be on stand-by once again, it's because, well, I don't give updates often enough :) Another bad thing is that I found out I wasted WAY too much time fixing all the problems that were occuring while displaying a background on the ListBox(script data window). So I finally deleted(well, cut/pasted) all the code related to this and flushed it out, so I can work on ScAIEdit III itself, and not lose my time on such futile details(all that time wasted, frustrating) ;) Oh well, everybody can do mistakes sometimes... Many people e-mailed me to know when the official(or first beta) release will be done. I only have one answer for now : "When it will be done"(I hope it's not copyrighted). As I described above, the pressure is already good enough, I don't need to add another one. However, I've got my personal date in mind which will remain secret, and I'm doing my best to get the first (most stable possible) beta release at that date. Don't worry, I can say this date is under... 1 or at worst 2 weeks :) Don't flame me if it's not out for this time though.

I know many of you reading this right now are in need to make some AI scripts, for their TC, or whatever, since I got lots of messages about this. I'll repeat it again, I'm doing my best to get it done, but I don't want to rush and release something even more buggy than the second version. I'm doing the project in a manner so the code stay clean, and readable. Some may wonder why it takes so much more time to get it done than it was on the I and II versions time, well the reason is simple. I now have more projects and other things to care about. I try to make the code clean so I won't have to do a fourth rewrite. The new version has a lot of new things to consider over the previous versions. See what I mean? :) Good. Oh god, got a lot of things written in that little box ;) I hope you haven't fallen asleep, but it compensates for the lack of updates on that :)

Thursday, February 24, 2000
Varous updates...King Arthur @ 19:36 EST

Even though I was lucky enough to get out of Writing class all this week, it still seemed to be quite busy for me. I've been doing a lot of work this week with Ius on the command-adding technology. Shadow sent in some especially good suggestions for new commands, and I hope to have them completed tonight or tomorrow:
  • Restock with energy / resource cost. Available on all vultures rather than just Raynor.
  • Energy attacks - require energy to attack. Useful for magical units that are otherwise defenseless, or for an increased effectiveness for an existing unit's attack. You could, for instance, research a really powerful attack at the Academy for Marines, and when the research is complete, all marines would get a new weapon and an energy bar. Maybe something like hypersonic rifles or something (ala the big guns in Aliens) - this last part was sort of my idea :)
  • Convert shields/ health to energy. Useful for the DA especially, who's got a crapload of shields and whose survival may depend on having enough energy to cast feedback or mind control
  • Convert energy to shields/health. Ditto for above
  • Unit summoning - he and I both realize this one will be difficult, I don't know if I will be able to do it. But I thought I should mention it so that you knew the idea was submitted.
Thanks also to the several other people (including one of the Aliens TC guys) for sending me in your ideas! Keep them coming.

Customs Uploader & Database CompleteKing Arthur @ 01:44 EST

As I mentioned before, nothing will show up on the database until it gets approved by an authorized CamSys staff member, Campaign Creations staff member, or an MVP (ala the forums). Customs Script UpdateKing Arthur @ 00:46 EST

I've been working pretty much non-stop the last couple of days to get the Customs Script finished. I have the uploading part complete, and tomorrow I hope to have the database (listing) portion complete as well. When finished, the script will let you upload any of the 4 different patch formats (PAT, CWAD, SECWAD, SEPAT), supply author information (with optional email) and a general title / description for the conversion. Thursday, February 10, 2000
StarGraft NewsKing Arthur @ 19:56 EST

I've decided that due to the last 17 or so odd buggy releases of StarGraft to stop development of the current version, and start over from scratch. The fundamental problem with the program is that, frankly, it was never meant for public consumption when I designed both the visual and the programming interface... I just basically made the program as quickly as possible, and with as little care as to where I threw code in. I really made no provisions for any easy expansion in the program, and as a result with the more options I add, and the more I try to fix it, the more hidden flaws surface. So starting whenever I have free time, I will be rewriting StarGraft completely from scratch. It probably won't take a long time, assuming that I have the necessary free time to do it in. I think it really needs to be done. So many people have kept complaining of the bugs that keep popping up in the latest "bugfix" that I've just had it, and I hope that this will make things easier on all of us. It'll save me from looking through a lot more code than I should be, and you from the unnecessary frustration of this flawed tool. StarGraft wouldn't be so hard to use, I think, if it actually worked reliably and had an intuitive interface, neither of which it seems to have for 99% of you. So my apologies, and please be patient as I re-develop it.

Sunday, February 06, 2000
CamSys joins the OrgNetworkKing Arthur @ 17:33 EST

I'm pleased to announce that Camelot Systems has officially joined the Org Network, a group of high quality fan sites including StarCraft.org and WarCraft.org. CamSys has had very close relations with the Org Network sites since their inception almost three (!!!) years ago, a few months before the release of StarCraft. Without a doubt, the Org Network is responsible for the popularity that CamSys has enjoyed in the last several years. They kept the community appraised of our latest innovations, and provided us with the much needed fan base to support our continued software development. About the only change that you will see is me posting on the Org sites from time to time with major CamSys news (such as software/tutorial releases), and an increased focus on all the games the Org Network sites cover (for now just Blizzard games). We will continue to design software for other games as the staff chooses, but the primary focus will be on Blizzard games.Hopefully this will launch a new era of success and cooperation for our site, and of course the Org Network. Thank you for your continued patronage, and your support/interest in our software.

Tutorial Progress is slow but sureKing Arthur @ 15:45 EST

My apologies that it's taking me a while to get the compiler (and associated tutorial) completed.
