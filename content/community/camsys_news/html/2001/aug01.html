Final Farewell - August 28, 2001 @ 6:38AM EDT - posted by KramerBoy

This news post has been quite a long time coming, I know, and those of you who have paid any attention to the sorry state of this website and my involvement in it over the past couple of years have probably been expecting this for a while.

Two years, seven months, and twenty-four days ago, I joined a flourishing Camelot Systems with as little skill as I had potential. Today, I find myself in much the same state. In nearly three years I managed to finish only two projects: SDTutor, a clumsy tutorial for a clumsy StarDraft, and ICE, a competent but needlessly complex editor for StarCraft's image scripts. Only one of those two remains available here at CamSys. In the gaping void between those two projects I managed to create nearly 100MB of programs and source code that never made it Camelot's surface; many you knew of, and many you didn't. As a last gesture of goodwill to those who have been loyal to our cause over the years, a list and description of all the projects I've undertaken that died in progress is available on the forums.

Today, Tuesday August 28, 2001, is the last day I will spend as a Camelot Systems staff member. Effective tomorrow I will no longer have anything to do with this website. I will stop reading the email, I will terminate the ICQ account, and I will never again post on this website or its forums. Except for the few close friends I have picked up along the way, none of you will ever hear from KramerBoy again. If you have something to say, say it now.

My reason for leaving is cold, but simple: I believe this website died when Andy Bond (King Arthur) left it. He was the creative drive and the talent behind what was Camelot Systems; the rest of us were just the support staff. When he left the website in my hands two summers ago, I was simply too inept to keep it afloat. Hosting problems that should have been resolved in a matter of weeks instead took months.

Our prolonged downtime coupled with a decline in our software's popularity drove away most of our dedicated fanbase. Case in point: We used to serve nearly 2,500 downloads a day - yesterday we served less than a quarter of that number. Since we resurfaced four months ago, no new projects have been completed, and only two programs have undergone any updates. In the entire month of August, only one news post has made it to our front page, and with the school season just beginning, it's only going to get worse.

To Andy and Cameron, thank you for looking past my sheer lack of skill and for taking the time to instruct me. Thank you for giving me such a great opportunity to expand my skills, and last but not least, thank you for that tour of Blizzard =).

To the rest of the Camelot Systems staff, I wish you all the best of luck and pray you can find a way to return this website to its former glory.

And to everyone who ever cared about Camelot Systems, I apologize for so mindlessly running it into the ground.

KB out.

Forward in the past (antithesis to Back to the Future) - August 11, 2001 @ 12:47AM EDT - posted by Veldhar[GD]

(Note: This post was to be displayed 2 days ago; however, due to recent server troubles caused by the move completion, the news script was down. Sorry for the inconvenience.)

Unless you didn't visit Camelot Systems for the past week, you've probably noticed many things have disappeared, like news posts, forum posts, new programs and such. Well, the server move completed, we were warned that there would be a loss of data between the backup time and the move time, so this is the result. However, I expected it to be a difference of a day or two instead of a full week. Oh well...

To summarize the news posts that have been deleted, Otak had added a new feature to the website, which is Replay of the Week. As its name suggests, it will feature a new replay each week that will surely be worth watching... or not ;) And as well, he released a new program for a game called Genders Wars, and obviously enough, it is named GenderDraft. It was available not so long ago, but the software had been deleted with the server move. Otak should re-upload it shortly.

I had also posted a news post (yeah, a page long or so), and since I don't have a backup of it, I can't repost it. So I'll try to summarize: I've been doing a giant catch-up on everything before leaving on a few days trip(which I'm back from). My lack of activity on the news was compensated by an intense work on two new projects, one that is quite big and game-related, the other is smaller but still hefty nonetheless, and is application-related. I'm not giving any other informations on these at the moment, but should be able to in a few weeks. The AI Tournament is still delayed due to myself being too busy with a little bit of everything, but it shouldn't be long before I post the official informations about it. Last of all, I've proceeded to create all remaining accounts for staff from the request e-mails I've received lately, so this matter is solved once and for all. As for the hosting requests, now that the server move is completed, I will be able to finalize the process (I couldn't request anything while our hosts were moving). You should see them appear very soon.

So this is the little summary for the news lost. Sorry about the forums that will be a week or so late in posts, and that were inaccessible for the past days due to lost configurations on the server-side, but this was to be expected. They are now ready to be used again. I hope this hasn't caused too much inconveniences.

Have fun!

- Veldhar[GD]
