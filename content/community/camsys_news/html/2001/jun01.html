A New Direction - June 22, 2001 @ 2:16AM EDT - posted by Defenderz

Camelot Systems started on WarCraft, then grew and thrived on Starcraft; however, with StarCraft fans slowly leaving, CamSys's community, while still much alive, is only a shadow of what it was a year ago. This problem was compounded with the fact that King Arthur left, and the website gained MAJOR hosting difficulties (leaving it offline for an elongated period of time). Blizzard has recognized this loss in users of StarCraft, and they have taken action with a new patch. While partially successful, even Replays (and Editors) can't bring us back to our previous peak, when StarCraft flourished. While the Replay Editor will be helpful for us, the answer in the long run is not in a new editor for StarCraft; we recognize that it alone will not push us up to a new peak. We must venture to other games in order to gain the plenary support we once had. Many of us have been wondering and debating what game(s) would be best to create customization for. I feel there is no better a way to find an answer to this question, then to ask the community, itself, what games it would like to have customization for. I would like to urge all of you who are reading this to e-mail me with suggestions for a game or games that you would like to see customized. Be as specific as you want in how it should be edited, or as generic as you wish. Any other suggestions will also be well noted. Thank you in advance for your e-mails which I hope will assist Camelot Systems in being what it once was, because hey, "Legends Never Die."

- Defenderz


Update on Replay Editor - June 21, 2001 @ 8:06PM EDT - posted by Rangsk

I'd like to start off by saying that people continue to email me saying that I should not create this replay editor because it would make all replay files illegitimate. I really wish people would read the complete post before emailing me. I will create a watermark/watermarks to ensure that people know that the replay files are edited. These will be very difficult to remove. Now that that's cleared up...

Blizzard has given me the green light for this project, as long as it cannot be used for cheating. Therefore, I cannot post the replay file format since someone could then create his (or her) own editor without a watermark. However, I believe that my editor will suit the needs of anyone who has a use for editing replay files, so no one will have a need for the spec sheets (and if it doesn't, feel free to drop me a line after I release the program and I will attempt to add the feature).

As for those who want an ETA for the replay editor, all I can say is "when it's done." I have been making progress, but it is slow work. It could be a week (not likely) or it could be 3 months. Only time will tell. Finally, I know many of you are extremely eager to help me. I'm grateful for the overwhelming support, but sometimes too much help isn't a good thing. Already people are stepping on each other's toes, so I regretfully must decline any further requests to help. However, I don't want to discourage you from attempting your own editor, as long as you ensure it cannot be used for cheating. I'll keep you updated as I make progress.


- Rangsk

Conclusion on Replay Editor - June 18, 2001 @ 2:57PM EDT - posted by Rangsk

Everyone look at Otak's update, yes, there is one down there somewhere :)

From what I can tell, there is a general consensus on the replay files. The editor could be really fun to use and a great asset to custom creators. Many people have emailed me saying, "Go for it" and saying that this project is one of the best ideas in a while. However, there is one concern almost everyone has pointed out. It would, in a second, totally ruin the whole use of replay files. One of the main purposes of replay files is to prevent hacking in games. If a person could simply edit a replay file, then who's to say who did the actual hacking. So, I have come to this conclusion. As a member of Camelot Systems and as a member of the Starcraft Community, I cannot and will not release a program that could allow a person to lie or cheat using replay files.

I will try my very best to add some sort of mark or marks to the replay file that make sure people know that it was edited. Also, this means that I cannot release information about how these replay files work (for obvious reasons). Mirroring what KA did with StarGraft, I will attempt to get in contact with Blizzard and get their feelings on the matter. If they say not to do it, then I, of course, will not do it. I would like your opinion on this matter. Should I forget the project now, should I continue and attempt to create a mark of some sort, or should I change the project to something else (please specify).

I would like to bring to your attention the way replay files work. As far as I know, they simply store unit locations and actions. Editing them would be consuming work. It would require putting in every single keystroke each person does at the proper time. This is fine for creating things such as movies, but for editing games the only real thing you could do would be to add text (I'm hoping to find some sort of way to do this, although it’s not a promise) and change character names/colors (which I may not include, for obvious reasons). So, please Email me with your opinion.


- Rangsk
