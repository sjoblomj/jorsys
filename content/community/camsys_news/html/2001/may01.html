Usually it's good news... but for us, it's another story. - May 19, 2001 @ 1:25AM EDT - posted by Veldhar[GD]

Well, the alpha test was leaked, so most of the Starcraft community was aware this would happen soon; the SC 1.08 patch has been finally released! With its new features and balances changes, it can be more or less considered as a refreshing wind on a game that's really starting to get old... but for the customization community, what does it means?

Usually, King Arthur and Merlin would immediately take their weapons and start tearing apart the new version to discover what is needed to make the customization softwares compatible with them; but as you all know, King Arthur has left Camsys and no one has ever got a sign of life from him ever since, and as for Merlin, the poor wizard is totally swamped with college work; hopefully, as he said, he should be able to reappear during the summer. But in the meanwhile, what?

The compatibility tests seem to be different for many; some have reported that StarDraft still works with 1.08 despite a few errors at the start, and others can't make it work at all. What is the verdict? Well... I'd like to bring mine, but I currently am on Windows 2000, which is incompatible with our patching softwares as you all know; sure, I am on dual boot, but the Windows 98 I have on this same disk is... somewhat... well, to summarize, I have trouble booting in it ^-^ Who knows what happened, I suspect some applications don't bother checking where the windows directory is and when uninstalling themselves, they delete win98 files instead of the win2k ones. The only valid explanation(and experimentation) I can get since I get a lot of missing files fatal errors if I try to boot. So to come back with the subject, I cannot verify the StarDraft compatibility by myself, not until I can somehow test it on the other computers here ;)

However, the second patching tool, StarGraft, has a unanimous verdict: it is totally incompatible(doh, it was to be expected as a EXE patcher). Immediately after, obviously, an avalanche of requests, demands and beggings are made to update it. Well, well, well... For the same reasons as StarDraft, it becomes extremely complicated to update it.

So what to do? KramerBoy has some experience with SD's source code, but I have no idea if he would be able to update StarDraft for 1.08, as it is quite different than just updating source code, but also patching with new numbers. I doubt anyone here has experience with StarGraft code either. As of me, I never looked into either of these program's source codes, thus I'm not sure of my ability of updating them. So to make a long story short, it becomes extremely complicated to make an update on these. Unless some staff here have extensive knowledge on any of these two softwares workings(if so, please let me know), or that Merlin becomes available sooner than expected, I don't suggest holding your breath for any updates. The best solution as of now is to maintain two versions of StarCraft, 1.07 and 1.08, one for customization and another one for your playing needs. The procedure is simple: before patching to 1.08, copy your Starcraft dir somewhere else. Apply the patch. Here you go, two Starcraft versions ;) If there's something you didn't understand, well... ask someone else to help! *chuckles*

So that should clear the matter about 1.08. As for Camsys? Well, I'm sure that you'll be pleased to know that Otak has been working on new pages, that is those about the chatroom and a few WarCraft 2 and Starcraft editing tutorials. I would have uploaded them tonight, but he isn't currently online. Oh well, just to let you know you should be seeing these soon! =) As for me, I should be working on a few specs pages in a matter of time, so expect these to be online soon too.

Another news concerning staff management: the staff list is now closed! While the current list doesn't mention of everyone(yet), I expect to be receiving e-mails containing COMPLETE informations(i.e. if you're missing the half of the fields, I'll ask you to resend your mail with all the infos this time) in the next few days, so you should see everyone aboard soon. Besides, I should update it tonight as well to reflect the newest changes. I also mentionned that the staff will be receiving the accounts they need when the staff list is going to be closed; well, this is done now. What next? Well, you guessed it =) It is time for all the staff to submit what you need! Here are the different accounts you can receive, according to what you'd like to have.

(This part concerns only Camsys staff)
  • E-mail: Every staff get it. Just mention the e-mail name you'd like to have, along with your e-mail password.
  • Special rank: As a Camsys staff, you are allowed to pick an original title as your BBS rank. If you are interested, just mention your account name and the title you'd like to receive.
  • News: If you can spell, are interested into posting and won't make utterly pointless posts, just mention your nickname with your news password.
  • #Camsys Auto-Op: If you come regularly to #Camsys, and are interested into operating the channel too, register your nickname at Nickserv and mention it. Note that you are subject to a few rules regarding the channel operating.
  • Moderator status: If you come regularly to the boards, and are interested into moderating them, mention your account name. Note that you are subject to a few rules regarding board moderating.
  • FTP access: This is a restrained access. If you can justify the use you'll make out of it(mention of your reasons), then you will get this account. Mention of your desired username and FTP password. Note that the FTP accounts won't be created until the CGI subdomain is up and running.

Once you have determined which accounts you need, just e-mail the account creation request with the required informations as showed above. Please note that you MUST use the same e-mail that you used to show a sign of life, as it is the only way I have to make sure you are from the staff. It should take a few days at most to get your accounts created after I receive your e-mails. As for FTP, for the reason mentionned above, it will take a bit longer.

That is all for today! Now that I have finished this college's session(and related things, just finished today), so I now have more free time at my disposition; for how long, I can't predict, but you should be seeing more movement within the next days :) Besides, I'll be uploading other Camsys softwares tonight, so watch the Software Live Wire grow a little as my 56k slowly finishes them ;)

P.S. I still haven't replied to e-mails, I apologize once again. But if you have unconstructive complaints, whining, or pointless e-mails, please send them to /dev/null. It will save me time for when I'll find some to reply. I'm really sorry to all those who have been waiting.

- Veldhar[GD]

What's wrong? Take off that look! - May 6, 2001 @ 12:52AM EDT - posted by Veldhar[GD]

Don't start blaming hallucinations, tireness or computer viruses, because it's real now; I know I have done much promises about getting Camsys back online VERY soon(tm) a few weeks ago, but college homeworks, finals preparation and a RL project had to get my attention first before I could work seriously on this coming back. Well, the finals are coming next week, so I expect my available time to be about as short, but at least I'm thinking of all of you who are still waiting patiently(and sometimes not ;)). Well, here it is!

We are now finally back online! So resitance is futile and all your base are belong to us. Err... You know what I mean ;) This new design has been nicely made by KramerBoy, and I have rewritten the HTML files to reduce the size of the design, so it works on 800x600 resolution too, and added up the contents. In case you have missed the official announcement made earlier on April, we are now heading for larger goals than just the customization of games; extending to this, we will be putting much focus on everything around as well. Concretely, that means everything 'game' on the technical side is aimed to be part of Camsys now. What brought the glory of Camsys 1.0 was the StarCraft customization tools, but we can't stay on that forever; while we will maintain a SC program development division(little or large, depends of staff interest), we will develop many other subjects now. Programming topics will obviously be maintained, as it is my intention to write up some articles on the subject, now that I barely have a program project for Camsys anymore ;) Merlin(yeah, he's alive, hard to believe isn't it? ;)) is also intending to do some writing this summer as well, and perhaps a few other Camsys staff as well if they're interested =)

Speak of which, I only have a little idea now of who's aboard! KramerBoy has done recruiting earlier on, but it looks like he didn't maintain a list of recruited staff, so I'm kinda on trouble keeping up with everyone. A short, non-exhaustive staff list has begun here, but I can be positive there is many more here. So if you were part of Camsys team, or received the confirmation letter from KB mentionning your application has been accepted, and that you are not listed, please contact me as soon as possible so I can get it updated and keep in touch with everyone in the team. DON'T try tricking me out, because I will ask for an official document in case I have any doubts, so if you can't justify or provide it, you'll be part of my next barbecue! As the meal, of course =)

A second message to the staff, if you want your accounts(news posting, e-mail, BBS moderator status, FTP access, depending of what your positions are about) set up, you need to contact me as well! You only have to provide me your username and password, and you'll get access in little time; isn't it nice? ;)

As of now, you probably noticed it, we have little contents activated right now! Well, it was either that or you wait one or two weeks more... you prefer? ;) Right now, I have uploaded what little my 56k has to offer me, that is StarDraft, StarGraft, ScAIEdit 3.1, and the BBS. You can expect more to be coming soon, as the rest of the softwares are fairly little so my modem takes them well. The rest of the main pages and of the design should be coming up in the next days/1-2 weeks too. So stay tuned, as we're not dead!

Many have answered my request for our softwares, and I thank everyone who has been kind enough to send me an e-mail and upload these somewhere so I can put them back online. Thanks goes to, in alphabetical order: [COH]AH, Cliff Adair, Dark Shadow, Defenderz, Domterran, Duran, Eric Feldbauer, Eric Peterson, Evan Quasney, Gabriel Matthews, KarlKFI, Jaguar, Krono, Le Duc Trung, Lucy Bahamondes Margery, Pikamaster, PSX, Sir Galahad and Suicidal Insanity, for helping me out retrieving our files =) This is my way to reply to all your e-mails, otherwise it would take me forever to write up all those ;) As for everyone else who sent me an e-mail, first, NO, I won't send you any of our softwares by e-mail; I'm not a living download server, so please be patient. For all other matters, you should receive a reply soon.

And, last of the news, in case you missed the announcements again, I'm now in charge of Camsys, but I guess it makes little difference for most of you ;) Still, I'll really do my best to at least post once in a while, and remain decently active on the boards. I'm eager to put up a full website soon, and, to all the staff, please contact me as mentionned above. I need all of you to restore Camsys! =)

- Veldhar[GD]
