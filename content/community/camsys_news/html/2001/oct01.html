L2 Edit - One of many new projects - October 19, 2001 @ 11:58PM EDT - posted by Otak

During the last 2 weeks or so, I have been working on a new project both for editing and practicing purposes for the upcoming Warcraft III: L2Edit. Its current function is to edit or create maps for the game Lords of the Realm 2 by Sierra. So far, not an incredable amount of progress has been done; I've only recently finished my work on the palettes, which I would like to thank Ace Calhoon for the great amount of help.

Lords2 is an old, but still popular medieval Role Time Stratagy for DOS, and Windows. For more details, visit Sierra's Website. Maps created with L2Edit should be able to work on multiplayer, but chances are the players will need to download the edited files/maps before being able to play. More information on this will show up as I progress. To view an example screen shot of the gameplay, I've uploaded a screen shot, using the palettes.

As for emailing me, since the site's email is still down, my other email address is Wizard_Otak@yahoo.com.

I'll keep you updated.

- Otak


It's time I say 'hi!' - October 11, 2001 @ 12:08PM EDT - posted by Sir Galahad

Well, well. It's been over 3.5 years now since I first joined Camelot Systems, so I guess it's time for me to make a post and say hi. Hi world! Those of you who visit the forum have probably seen me posting there occasionally and a few others might have spotted me in the #CamSys channel on Dal.net (Which is still pretty empty, considering the amount of visitors on our forum and the counter on the downloads... Don't be shy, just hop in). But I can hear the rest think "Who are you?". Well, here goes:

In the early days of Camelot Systems, when StarCraft wasn't around yet (and seen by most of us as just a silly game of WarCraft in space), and the core of the editing community was still hanging around on the WarDraft forum, King Arthur attracted me, among others as a Support Technician for Camelot Systems, to give solutions to the problems people would have and post on the forums or email us directly. In addition to that, I would do a bit of data decrypting (sounds clever, but it just means finding out what the data in certain files actually meant) and a bit of programming. A few utilities for WarCraft II came from my hand, but troubles with my internet connection prevented me from carrying out my Tech Support part out to the fullest extend until much later. Although Camelot has evolved a bit from time to time, I just sticked around somewhere in a corner and am still a proud member.

Well, that's enough rambling about the past for one post, now on to the current times. As Defenderz has posted recently, the @camsys.org emails currently do not work correctly. Since this tend to take quite a while, it might be usefull for those with problems out there to know the alternative email addresses of the Support Technicians (Namely Ace Calhoon and myself) until the @camsys.org email addresses get back to work. You can email me at thels@wanadoo.nl and Ace Calhoon at ace_calhoon@hotmail.com. You can also use ICQ at 20800959 (me) or 9329218 (Ace Calhoon). Lastly, you might want to know what I'm up to nowadays. Well, it's called StarXed, named after the first unofficial map editor for WarCraft 2, namely War2Xed. StarXed will hopefully one day be an all around editor for StarCraft Scenario's, that should become an alternative to using StarEdit in combination with Emerald Patches. It's going to take quite a while before things get there though. I'll keep you informed.

- Sir Galahad


Good News and Bad News - October 5, 2001 @ 4:57PM EDT - posted by Quantam

Okay, first the good news. I finally got the last 2 known crashes fixed on MPQDraft. It took me so long because only 2 of the 50 people using MPQDraft (and I was not one of the 2) ever saw these crashes, so I didn't have much to go on. But, MPQDraft is feature-complete (actually it was feature complete in the last beta), and I'm about to release another beta to my testers. If no more serious bugs are found, it should be released publicly in a couple weeks.

Now the bad news, regarding the MP3/MIDI plugin for MPQDraft (officially named ThunderGraft). I was working on that project with Dark Brood from SC3D. In fact, he was doing about 2/3 of the work for it. But, he just suffered a hard drive crash, and lost all his work on it, in addition to some important school stuff. This does not mean the project is cancelled, it just means that it will be greatly delayed, as he has to rewrite it from scratch. But first he has to work manically to remake the school stuff he lost before it's due. I'll keep you posted on our progress.

- Quantam
