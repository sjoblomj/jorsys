Another year, another revolutionary program
Posted on January 2, 2002 @ 4:14AM GMT-5 by Quantam
Haaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaappy New Year! Well, it's January 1st, so it must be that time again. That's right, it's time for me to release another program! And do I ever have a good one for you this time. I proudly present to you MPQDraft, the biggest step forward in mod making since MoPaQ 2000.

For those of you who have not heard about MPQDraft while it was in development and later in beta, it is a patch loader similar to StarDraft, even able to create self-executing patches. However, MPQDraft is far more than StarDraft ever was, and will hopefully be the fatal blow to StarDraft once and for all (as a Win2K user I do not like StarDraft). The major advantages of MPQDraft over StarDraft:
- Works on any version of Windows
- Works on any version of Starcraft, as well as any version of Diablo, Diablo 2 Warcraft 2 BNE, and any Blizzard program that uses MPQs (and I'm hoping it will also work on Warcraft 3)
- Has NO slowdown, as StarDraft used to
- Has NO files which it cannot patch (like StarDraft couldn't patch music files)
- Has no known bugs at this time (after being beta-tested for a while)
- But perhaps the biggest advantage is that MPQDraft supports patching plugins (i.e. StarGraft will be a plugin soon)

However, unlike StarDraft, MPQDraft does not include a patch creator. MPQDraft runs MPQ patches created by MPQ2K. In addition, MPQDraft will also run Diablo 2 Mod Manager mods (D2M files) just as well. But, unlike a normal mod created with MPQ2K, you do not need to replace patch_rt.mpq or ANY existing MPQ. You can even run multiple patches at once.

Anyways, enough babbling. Click here to download MPQDraft (Yes I know it doesn't have a manual yet. I'll get to it)
