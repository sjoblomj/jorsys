Camelot Staff Openings!
Posted on January 16, 2002 @ 5:14AM GMT-5 by Veldhar[GD]
It took time, but it's finally done. After fixing most of the major bugs that were found in the website and reorganizing Camelot, the Camelot Castle is now ready to be populated by a full complement of staffers!

Were you part of the previous Camelot dynasty and are motivated to get back in the crew to produce good work? Are you an experienced programmer or technical support expert and wish to get in on the action of a reknowned game customization and creation community? Is the taste of challenge and fan mail reward enough for your professional work? Then don't hesitate, and apply now!

The available staff positions are:

Game Debugger
The x86 ASM language has no secret for you, and staring at whole assembly code pages to decode them is part of your numerous skills. Disassembly or SoftICE experience is a must. Knowledge of C/C++ is a good advantage, but not necessary.

Game Programmer
You are highly talented in the programming field and have an excellent knowledge of C/C++ or a similar language. Possessing skills in gaming APIs like DirectX, OpenGL or equivalents is a must. You are very motivated in your projects. Knowing how to use the x86 ASM language efficiently is facultative, but a considerable bonus.

Game Editor Programmer
You are experienced and comfortable using an High Level Language such as C++ Builder, Delphi, and/or Visual C++. You can easily create good user interfaces to allow the edition of existing game data. The possession of file deciphering skills is of a big help. Win32 and VCL usage is majorly preferred over MFC.

Note to programmers: Having your own project ongoing is a MAJOR bonus, as our current project pool is limited. Additionally, you must be able to provide some example of your past works as an e-mail attachment, or accessible URL.

Technical Support Expert
You really like to help others, and answering to technical support questions is part of your pastime, and the users appreciation, your reward. You know fairly well how to use the gaming software Camelot Systems is providing, and can help others to use them. Being able to write tutorials and help files is a big bonus.

It is also to be noted that all positions require a fairly good amount of free time. After all, you're working here! :)

If one of these positions interests you and think you can meet the exigences, then we are waiting for you! Send an e-mail to hirings@camsys.org, and include the following informations:

- Real name, age
- Thorough description of your skills
- Past works example, as e-mail attachment or URL (N/A for Tech Support Experts)
- Why do you want to get in Camsys?
- What do you intend to be doing?

Due to the potentially high demand and number of applications, it might not be possible to reply to all of them; the selected persons will be contacted back by e-mail with a positive reply. Please accord us 1-2 weeks to review your submission.

Thanks in advance for your participation and interest!
