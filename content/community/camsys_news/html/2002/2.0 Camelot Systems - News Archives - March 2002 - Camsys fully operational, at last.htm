Camsys fully operational, at last
Posted on March 17, 2002 @ 1:05AM GMT-5 by Veldhar[GD]
There has been several problems into contacting our hosts, but once it has been done, everything went well and fast; the server is now fully operational without a single problem.

However, the website has not been put back online right after the fix. Why? The reason is simple: confusion still reigns as to the heading of Camelot Systems for the next months. With the arrival of WarCraft 3 Beta, the wave of excitation surrounding it gave one of the last final blows to StarCraft. But what about WarCraft 3?

Most of the Camsys community has expected Camelot to live back with the arrival of WarCraft 3; however, much has changed since all these years. The StarCraft modding community has been absolutely successful at proving Blizzard the power and influence that customization has on the lifetime of a great classic game. This move has been heard, and WarCraft 3 has been made surprisingly customizable; even with just its beta looks.

Nothing is known about WorldEdit yet, but according to the files specifications we have looked through, very little seems impossible for now.

  • The scripting language is most likely the best thing that could have happened to any dreaming modder; as was promised, you can do about anything you want, when you want, in your maps. Forget everything about the StarCraft triggers system; there's now a real programming language coming with WarCraft III. No need to say things like custom campaigns, AI development or RPG maps making will be aeons ahead of whatever StarCraft had.

  • Without any doubts, all units will be able to have new balance statistics and new names, as was allowed by StarEdit before. Additionally, many other statistics should be modifiable too; there is a dazingly number of fields found in the excel files used for units in WarCraft 3, like the base amount of gold/lumber a worker can carry, the capacity of certain units/buildings, or the circle radius the acolytes form around a gold mine while extracting its gold. All modifiable very easily through a single number or data value.

  • The topic of new units is still rather complex to elaborate at this early point. It is almost sure every unit model will be skinnable through the use of texture images using a custom JPEG format. As for model themselves, if and only if Blizzard decides to support their customization, it will most likely be within the form of a 3ds max plugin. No need to say the sum of work required would probably be quite more heavy than what SC had, though.

  • Custom campaigns should be much more supported by the game itself now, by allowing custom cinematics and ingame scenes, as well as automatic map cycle campaign handling, most likely handled by the scripting language itself. No need to say it should be much more easier to make complete campaigns featuring even non-linear plots by the time WarCraft 3 is released. No need to also say WarCraft 3 campaigns will most likely take advantage of this too.

So, with all these exciting new features found within WarCraft 3, most likely all benefitting to every modder out there, what about Camelot Systems? Unfortunately, this means that we'll have very little, if anything at all to do with WarCraft 3. The only point where we might have to intervene are models, for new models might not be supported directly by WorldEdit. Fortunately, it should be quite possible to make a 3rd party 3ds max plugin to export to WarCraft 3 models, as I have been able to decipher the model files quite well with minimal problems. However, for the rest, there shouldn't be much need for any important 3rd party tool like StarCraft had; perhaps small side tools, but nothing as important as StarDraft or StarGraft. Additionally, the WarCraft 3 Beta License Agreement prevents any such modification tool by the NDA clause, which means that no release or progress news can be made until the retail version at least hits the shop shelves (to be grabbed seconds later), which won't be happening for at least a few other months. In other words, Camelot Systems doesn't have much future with WarCraft 3.

The following question is quite obvious: "So what now?". Camelot Systems has been created and has lived by providing many customization tools for the popular and classic games WarCraft II and StarCraft. Things have been changing over and over during the past year and a half, without any success. WarCraft III might not be the long-awaited solution. "So what now?" This is what our brainstorming is about. More to come later, most likely.

In the mean time, have fun, and enjoy the WarCraft III beta if you're one of the few and proud Beta Testers! If not, feel free to participate in our forums and chatroom located on DAL.net in #camsys. The downloads are also finally brought back online with the website comeback, so for everyone who has been waiting for them, don't wait any longer!

Until the next time...
