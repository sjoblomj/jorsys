The Camelot castle awakens for WarCraft III
Posted on July 9, 2002 @ 3:44PM GMT-5 by Veldhar[GD]
A month or two ago, Camelot Systems has deemed WarCraft III not to be a viable path to deliver customization tools for it as it was done for StarCraft, given the dramatically increased power of their map editor, WorldEdit.

Less than a week after its public release, WarCraft III is meeting the expectations, and more. The campaigns are well-thought and beautiful, multiplayer games have great gameplay, and the computer AI is invincible (as far as we know, no one has been able to beat it on 1v1 yet). The map editor is very good, albeit a bit awkward on its interface, but most importantly, the JASS script language (triggers) unveils an incredible power to any map maker who knows how to make proper use of it.

But even after all this, it is still possible to expect third party tools to deliver even more new features to WarCraft III. How so? Among things, custom textures and custom models are not supported by WorldEdit, which, even with the huge amount of features it provides, is still a great lack to customization.

For this reason, Camelot Systems is currently working hard on texture and model conversion tools to push WarCraft III customization bounds even further than originally intended. Further topics of customization possibilities are also being explored currently.

You can expect such tools to be released shortly, as a lot of progress has been made in the past few days. Here is a screenshot as for a sample of what it is possible to do so far with such tools:

Now, where has the sheep gone?

In the meantime, have fun with WarCraft III! It is a game that was extremely worth the wait, both in terms of playing and customization. Stay tuned to Camelot Systems for further news on the tools development!
