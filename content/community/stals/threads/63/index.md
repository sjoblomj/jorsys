I would like to say that the point of a  
Warcraft II page is mainly to show other  
people strategies. In my page I am currently  
making/putting a lot in, and this is now what  
I am doing completely. I first was adding  
all this "crap" to my page, but now I know.

I have seen War2 pages where the webmaster  
doens't even know how to play Warcraft II.  
What's the point of making a page and praising  
a game without even knowing what it is?!?

If you plan on having (or already have) don't  
go around just adding stuff that's not really  
important, even though it is fun (I definitely  
know; first-hand experiences). Make strategies,  
and more people will come! Just look at  
Shlonglor's page. Thousands of people come A DAY!!!  
He has some fun stuff in there, yes he does, but  
look at those strategies! Whoa!

MAY WARCRAFT II, THE BEST GAME IN THE WORLD, LIVE  
F O R E V E R! ! !
