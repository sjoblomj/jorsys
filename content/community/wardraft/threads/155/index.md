I have recently observed that the Red color that cycles for the Circle of Power will not cycle when used in graphics other than the Circle unless the Circle is present in the same Pud. This implies that either the Circle of Power has a unique attribute or the game actually checks to see whether or not a Circle is present. In either case, if anyone knows how to activate the Circle color Cycling without placing a circle, please tell how.  
  
Also, I would advise anyone who has created a graphic with Red that is not for team designation check their graphic in a Pud with the Circle in it to ensure that their stuff will not flash later.  
  
Fuller
