# New Freeware Game in development -- Blasted Wastes

Blasted Wastes is a new, fantasy era game which is a mix between civilization and Warcraft II. It is being written by a group of net users from around the world. We require good graphics artists who can help us do the animation sprites of the units, buildings, heroes, etc. Of course, anyone is welcome to help, but the least developed area of the game is graphics.  
None of the following i  
  
The idea of the game is that the player will select a race from those six available and for each different race, there will be a certain number of preset missions in which the player must play against the computer. One different thing which will defer from Warcraft II is that the player will have a special character which will be fighting with the other units in the game. If the player’s special character dies, then the game ends.  
  
The game will be divided into two modes:  
  
Management: (similar to Civilization)  
This part will enabled the player to build dwellings for the settlements, hire / fire units (from the same or different friendly races), mine gold, harvest food... etc. The player will manage the lands he has conquered from the strategy part. The more land (and settlements) are conquered, the more people will be available for training as soldiers, and the more gold and food will be available.  
  
During this phase of the game, the player will also need to manage the number of units defending each conquered land, because if there will be enemy lands adjacent to player’s land, then it will be possible that the land will be conquered by the computer if there aren’t enough troops to defend it.  
  
The more the player conquers land, the more he will gain experience, leadership and charisma. This will help the player hire units much more easily. It will also help the player get new allies from the other races. Note that the good guys only ally with the good guys, and the bad guys with the bad guys. If allies from the other races join the player, then the player can hire units from the new allied races.  
  
Strategy: (similar to Warcraft II)  
This is the part where the player will be pitted against the computer in a pre-set mission, and he will need to complete the mission, in order to conquer the land and the settlements. The player will be able to call build structures so that troops can be trained, and also to call for reinforcements from other settlements (which have been conquered by the player).  
  
Missions will vary from just constructing a settlement, defending a settlement, eliminating the enemy from the territory, to destroying the chief bad guy in his own land. Initially, the first couple levels will be constructing settlements and / or reinforcing settlements. This will help the player get more gold and hire more units in the management mode between the strategy phases.  
  
  
Human: The Human race is the most common race found in the Blasted Wastes world. The Humans are peaceful, but can become ferocious when required. Humans are also more social and tolerant than most other races, excepting the company of elves, dwarves, and the like with noticeably less complaint. Because of these abilities and tendencies, humans have become significant powers within the world and often rule empires that other races would find difficult to manage.  
Elf: Elves tend to be somewhat taller and slimmer than normal humans. They are not fond of ships or mines. They do not make friends easily. They prefer to distance themselves from humans, have little love for Dwarves, and hate the evil denizens of the woods. They find magic and swordplay fascinating.  
  
Dwarf: Dwarves are short, stocky fellows, easily identified by their size and shape. Dwarves tend to be dour and taciturn. They are strong and brave, like the earth and dislike the sea. Not overly fond of elves, they have a fierce hatred of orcs and goblins. They are ill-disposed toward magic and have little talent for it, but revel in fighting, warcraft, and scientific arts such as engineering. Dwarves typically dwell in hilly or mountainous regions.  
Orc: The Orcs are short, green-skinned and ugly. They are stupid (except for their warlocks) and ferocious. The few smart ones are their leaders (Ledorijs) and warlocks (Orothogs). Orcs are usually used as cannon fodder by the other evil races, but sometimes a particularly smart leader rises to the top, and the orcs become independent. The only reason the orcs are able to be a threat to the other races is their immensely fast breeding rate. The sheer numbers of the orcs is enough to topple many smarter races, especially with a good leader advised by a warlock.  
Undead: The Undead are a race of dead people who have revived by Vampire Lords. They are difficult to kill, since if there is a Vampire Lord nearby, they will be easily regenerated. The Undead tend to live in caves and deep tunnels inside cavernous mountains.  
Qabaran: The Qabaran hordes are big war-bands of mixed evil races trying to conquer the land occupied by the Human race and its allies. Qabaran hordes are extremely violent and merciless. They just pillage and destroy whatever comes into their path. To the other races of the land, the Qabarans are known as Desert Spawn, because they were first seen ingering around the Desert of Bones.  
  
Development Requirements:  
  
The game will be coded in Borland Pascal 7.0 under DOS 6.22 or Windows 95 operating systems. We'll be using various utilties for drawing 2D and 3D graphics, and also MIDI/WAV/VOC editors.
